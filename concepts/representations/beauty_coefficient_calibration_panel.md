# Beauty-coefficient calibration panel

## Summary

A four-case visual calibration set shows how the beauty coefficient behaves for high, low, zero, and negative delayed-recognition trajectories.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, annual citation history, awakening year, beauty coefficient, reference line, or exemplar case.
- Typical representation: diagnostic panel of annual citation histories with reference line and awakening marker.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: interpretability of the beauty coefficient across trajectory classes.
- Empirical signature: example histories span bursty delayed recognition, weak awakening, immediate peaking, and above-reference-line negative values..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines beauty-coefficient interpretation by linking it to [beauty coefficient](../measures/beauty_coefficient.md) and [citation history reference line](citation_history_reference_line.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [awakening time](../measures/awakening_time.md) is interpreted from citation histories.
- Adds cross-links to [negative beauty coefficient trajectory class](../measures/negative_beauty_coefficient_trajectory_class.md) so delayed-recognition and ranking motifs stay connected.

## Operationalization

- Plot annual citations with the beauty-coefficient reference line and awakening marker.
- Use exemplar cases to show high B, low B, B equals zero, and negative B behavior.
- Pair the panel with the formula so users can see what each trajectory shape contributes to B.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Ke et al. (2015) uses four APS citation histories to calibrate how B separates long sleep plus burst, weak low-volume awakening, immediate peak, and negative above-reference-line trajectories.
- The panel makes a scalar delayed-recognition score inspectable through concrete citation histories.

## Caveats

- The examples are illustrative rather than a sampling frame.
- Visual calibration depends on the chosen cases and axis scaling.
