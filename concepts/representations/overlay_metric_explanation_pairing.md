# Overlay metric explanation pairing

## Summary

Overlay metric explanation pairing uses science-map overlays as explanatory companions to scalar indicators, showing which field relations create or limit a measured score.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: subject category, field-year profile, paper set, portfolio, scalar indicator, or map overlay.
- Typical representation: indicator-plus-map pair, overlay comparison, node-size and distance explanation, or metric interpretation panel.
- Representation target: make the field structure behind a scalar metric visible.
- Empirical signature: maps show the variety, balance, and disparity behind interdisciplinarity indicators and confirm or qualify scalar rankings.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [field-year cited subject-category overlays](field_year_cited_sc_overlay.md).
- Provides the representation layer for [interdisciplinarity metric-map triangulation](../validations/interdisciplinarity_metric_map_triangulation.md).
- Helps manage [science-map projection readability tradeoffs](../validations/science_map_projection_readability_tradeoff.md) by pairing visual and scalar evidence.
- Generalizes to research profiling, science intelligence, and evaluation dashboards.

## Operationalization

- Compute scalar indicators for the focal paper set, field, or portfolio.
- Project the same category or citation distribution onto a science map.
- Encode variety as active nodes, balance as relative node size, and disparity as map distance.
- Compare scalar differences with overlay patterns.
- Use maps to explain why a metric changes, stays flat, or differs across fields.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Porter and Rafols (2009) states that interdisciplinarity indicators guide trends but do not detail the areas on which a subject category draws.
- The authors explain that science maps can directly show variety, balance, and disparity through nodes, node sizes, and distances.
- They use science maps to visually confirm indicator-based comparisons between subject categories and state that overlays enrich understanding of interdisciplinarity metrics.

## Caveats

- Overlay interpretation inherits map projection and distance-matrix assumptions.
- Visual confirmation can be persuasive even when the map is approximate.
- Pairing maps with metrics helps explanation but does not validate category assignments by itself.
