# Romans-Polchinski extreme case record

## Summary

A structured single-case record captures the paper identified as the most extreme sleeping beauty in the study.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: sleeping paper, waking paper, field, sleep length, sleep depth, wake intensity, trigger year, or actor comment.
- Typical representation: case-record schema for an extreme sleeping-beauty trajectory.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: interpretive validation of a rare delayed-recognition case.
- Empirical signature: the case record links the sleeping paper, first post-sleep citing paper, and proposed mechanism..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines sleeping-beauty case representation by linking it to [awakening trigger citation](../mechanisms/awakening_trigger_citation.md) and [expert comment prematurity validation](../validations/expert_comment_prematurity_validation.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [ranked sleeping beauty case inventory](ranked_sleeping_beauty_case_inventory.md) is interpreted from citation histories.
- Adds cross-links to [ranked sleeping beauty case inventory](ranked_sleeping_beauty_case_inventory.md) so delayed-recognition and ranking motifs stay connected.

## Operationalization

- Store sleeping paper, publication year, field, sleep-depth values, wake intensity, first post-sleep citing paper, trigger year, actor comments, and proposed awakening mechanism.
- Use the record to audit whether the candidate is a true delayed-recognition case rather than a data artifact.
- Keep single-case records distinct from population prevalence estimates.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) identifies L. J. Romans's 1986 supergravity paper with cw equals 64 and J. Polchinski's 1995 paper as the first citing paper after ten years.
- The narrative also records how the link was made and why the field became ready.

## Caveats

- Single-case records are useful for interpretation and validation but should not be generalized without population evidence.
- Mechanism claims depend on narrative evidence and author recollection.
