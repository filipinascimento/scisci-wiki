# Institutional pathways to the frontier

## Summary

Institutional pathways to the frontier represent the institutional composition of multi-step citation paths that connect patents or papers to the patent-paper boundary.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: citation path, patent, paper, institution type, D=1 boundary node, or path step.
- Typical representation: path diagram or path-type distribution from distant outputs through intermediary patents and papers to the boundary.
- Representation target: show which institutional actors mediate indirect connections to science or technology.
- Empirical signature: distant patents often reach the patent-paper frontier through particular institution-type sequences.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [integrated patent-paper citation networks](integrated_patent_paper_citation_networks.md) with institution-type attributes.
- Complements [university-firm patent-paper handoff](../mechanisms/university_firm_patent_paper_handoff.md) by representing indirect rather than only direct D=1 handoffs.
- Helps interpret [science-technology distance](../measures/science_technology_distance.md) as a sequence of institutional links.

## Operationalization

- Compute shortest paths from focal patents or papers to the patent-paper boundary.
- Attach institution-type labels to each patent or paper node where possible.
- Aggregate path patterns by starting institution, boundary institution, and intermediary institution types.
- Compare direct boundary handoffs with indirect pathways.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Ahmadpoor and Jones (2017) reports that other patents typically connect to the patent-paper frontier through D=1 firm patents.
- The supplementary figures describe institutional pathways for patents reaching the frontier.
- This representation makes the boundary a path-mediated institutional system rather than only a direct university-to-firm handoff.

## Caveats

- Shortest paths can omit alternative meaningful pathways.
- Institution-type labels can be incomplete or noisy.
- A citation path documents traceability, not necessarily direct knowledge transfer or contractual handoff.
