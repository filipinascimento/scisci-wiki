# Altmetric complementary-impact frame

## Summary

Altmetric complementary-impact frame is the representation of altmetrics as complements to citation indicators for traces of attention beyond scholarly citation impact.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, altmetric source, citation indicator, nonacademic attention trace, or impact narrative.
- Typical representation: complementary indicator panel, impact-channel map, or citation-plus-altmetric dashboard.
- Representation target: assign different evidentiary roles to scholarly citations and alternative attention traces.
- Empirical signature: altmetrics are interpreted as additional attention channels rather than replacements for citation impact.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Frames [altmetric residual-category caveat](../validations/altmetric_residual_category_caveat.md) as a role-assignment problem.
- Connects [citation impact indicators](../measures/citation_impact_indicators.md) to [public and policy attention](../mechanisms/public_policy_attention.md).
- Supports [responsible metrics](../measures/responsible_metrics.md) by preventing single-indicator substitution.
- Helps policy-mention studies explain why sparse policy traces still matter descriptively.

## Operationalization

- Display citation indicators and altmetric indicators in separate channels.
- State what kind of attention each source can and cannot evidence.
- Avoid aggregating altmetrics and citations into one unqualified impact score.
- Pair altmetric counts with context audits and source-coverage notes.
- Use target-oriented panels when the intended audience is policy, public, clinical, educational, or industrial.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Haunschild and Bornmann (2017) frames altmetrics as traces that can complement citation analysis by capturing attention outside science.
- The paper does not treat policy mentions as mature replacements for citation-based indicators.
- This motif captures the role assignment between citation indicators and altmetric channels.
- It is distinct from source coverage caveats because it asks how the indicators should be represented together.

## Caveats

- Complementarity does not imply validity; each channel still needs source, context, and denominator checks.
- Some altmetric sources reflect scholarly rather than nonacademic attention.
- Composite dashboards can still invite misuse if users collapse channels informally.
