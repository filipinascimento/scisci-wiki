# Nobel laureate career-publication links

## Summary

Laureate-paper link tables distinguish prize-winning papers from other career publications for Nobel laureates.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: Nobel laureate, publication, prize-winning paper flag, scientific domain, or primary paper ID.
- Typical representation: laureate career publication panel linked to primary paper identifiers.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: recognition-linked publication histories for scientific Nobel laureates.
- Empirical signature: a linked table marks laureate publications and identifies prize-winning publication types..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Provides a dataset layer for [Nobel-prize credit validation](../validations/nobel_prize_credit_validation.md).
- Links recognition studies to [paper-family canonicalization](../methods/paper_family_canonicalization.md).
- Supports career-impact and recognition-prediction analyses.

## Operationalization

- Integrate laureate publication records.
- Map affiliated paper IDs to primary paper IDs.
- Retain laureate ID and prize-winning type flags.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Lin et al. (2023) describes integrating a 1900-2016 laureate publication dataset and mapping it to SciSciNet primary IDs.

## Caveats

- Coverage is limited to the source laureate dataset and represented Nobel domains.
- Prize-winning paper flags can simplify complex recognition histories.
