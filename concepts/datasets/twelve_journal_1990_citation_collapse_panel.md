# Twelve-journal 1990 citation-collapse panel

## Summary

Twelve-journal 1990 citation-collapse panel is a cross-journal cohort of papers used by Wang et al. to test whether rescaled citation histories collapse beyond the Physical Review corpus.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, journal cohort, publication year, or citation trajectory panel.
- Typical representation: fixed-year multi-journal panel, rescaled citation history, or cross-journal validation cohort.
- Dataset target: test whether a citation-history model generalizes across prominent journals.
- Empirical signature: rescaled trajectories from journals with different citation practices collapse onto a shared curve.

- Provides an external validation panel for [universal citation-history rescaling](../validations/universal_citation_history_rescaling.md).

## Operationalization

- Select all papers from a fixed publication year across multiple journals.
- Extract long-run citation histories.
- Fit paper-level parameters and rescale trajectories.
- Compare collapse quality across journal cohorts.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wang et al. (2013) tests papers published in 1990 by 12 prominent journals.
- The paper reports a Science 1990 cohort of 842 papers in the figure caption.
- The validation sits beside the Physical Review collapse test, making it a cross-journal robustness panel.

## Caveats

- The panel is fixed to one publication year.
- Prominent journals may not represent lower-visibility venues or all fields.
- Citation database coverage and journal selection rules should be recorded before reuse.
