# Novelty-sensitive indicator portfolio

## Summary

Evaluation of novel work should combine long-window, indirect-impact, distributional, and venue-residual evidence rather than depend on JIF or early citations.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: evaluation dossier, novel paper, indicator dashboard, or funding decision.
- Typical representation: multi-indicator portfolio for novelty-sensitive assessment.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: reducing novelty bias in bibliometric evaluation.
- Empirical signature: novel work is assessed with long-window and indirect indicators plus distributional risks rather than only early citations or journal prestige..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Turns [novelty penalty](../mechanisms/novelty_penalty.md) into an assessment-design recommendation.
- Connects [metrics support qualitative assessment](metrics_support_qualitative_assessment.md) to novelty evaluation.
- Useful for grant, hiring, and institutional evaluation of exploratory work.

## Operationalization

- Build dashboards with novelty class, multiple citation windows, cited-by-big-hit status, JIF residuals, low-tail and top-tail risk measures, and qualitative context.
- State which indicators are decision aids rather than targets.
- Audit dashboard use for gaming and decision overload.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wang et al. (2017) warns against standard bibliometric indicators for novel work and recommends a wider indicator portfolio.
- The recommendation follows from observed short-window and venue penalties.

## Caveats

- Indicator proliferation can create gaming or decision overload unless metrics remain decision aids.
- Novelty-sensitive portfolios still require expert judgment.
