# Diversity-performance trade-off frontier

## Summary

Diversity-performance trade-off frontier maps how conditionally optimal portfolio composition changes as more weight is placed on diversity relative to immediate option performance.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: portfolio, option mix, diversity weight, performance score, stakeholder perspective, or policy scenario.
- Typical representation: frontier of portfolio shares across a diversity-performance trade-off parameter.
- Method target: explore the consequences of valuing diversity, interactions, and individual option performance under alternative assumptions.
- Empirical signature: the recommended portfolio moves from high-performing concentrated options toward more diverse mixtures as the diversity weight increases.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Operationalizes [diversity-premium trade-off](../mechanisms/diversity_premium_tradeoff.md) for research governance and funding portfolios.
- Extends [diversity measures](../measures/diversity_measures.md) from descriptive indicators to portfolio-design tools.
- Supports deliberation about research programs, technology pathways, field portfolios, or institutional strategies under uncertainty.
- Connects [portfolio-interaction diversity value](portfolio_interaction_diversity_value.md) to policy-relevant optimization and sensitivity analysis.

## Operationalization

- Define candidate portfolio elements, their current or possible shares, and performance criteria.
- Construct a [perspective-dependent disparity space](../representations/perspective_dependent_disparity_space.md) and any portfolio interaction terms.
- Choose a system-value expression that combines individual option performance with portfolio-level diversity value.
- Vary the diversity trade-off parameter over a wide range.
- For each parameter value, optimize portfolio shares and plot the resulting composition frontier.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Stirling (2007) presents a system-value heuristic that combines individual element performance with diversity and interaction-adjusted portfolio value.
- The paper uses sensitivity analysis to vary the weight attached to diversity and traces conditionally optimal systems across that trade-off.
- Stirling illustrates the method with habitat and energy-portfolio examples, showing how portfolio composition changes as more value is placed on diversity.
- The full text emphasizes that the purpose is not an unconditional optimum but systematic exploration of assumptions and perspectives.
- The value-model inputs and deliberative workflow are split into [performance-space value representation](../representations/performance_space_value_representation.md) and [deliberative diversity sensitivity analysis](deliberative_diversity_sensitivity_analysis.md).

## Caveats

- Optimization results can be driven by subjective performance weights, disparity dimensions, and interaction assumptions.
- A frontier is an aid to deliberation, not a direct prescription unless the value model has been agreed.
- Data gaps and uncertainty should be shown alongside the frontier, especially for research-policy decisions.
