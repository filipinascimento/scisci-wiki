# WoS machine-learning training extract permission

## Summary

Some WoS extracts may be licensed specifically for training statistical or machine-learning software to identify patterns.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: data extract, model-training corpus, algorithm, licensed use case, or derived model artifact.
- Typical representation: data-use scope label separating model training, analysis, and deployment.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: permitted algorithm-training uses of bibliometric extracts.
- Empirical signature: a data extract is explicitly scoped for training or pattern-detection software, with credit and use restrictions..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds model-training scope to [bibliometric data access modes](bibliometric_data_access_modes.md).
- Important for [automated predictive-evaluation safeguards](../validations/automated_predictive_evaluation_safeguards.md).
- Connects fit-for-purpose extracts to ML lineage and output restrictions.

## Operationalization

- Label extracts as model-training, direct analysis, or production-integration data.
- Store permitted algorithm scope, attribution terms, and output restrictions.
- Keep training, validation, and deployment data lineage separate.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Birkle et al. (2020) states that academic research use can include extracts for training software using mathematical and statistical algorithms, subject to terms and credit.

## Caveats

- Permission to train does not imply unrestricted redistribution of raw data or model artifacts.
- Model weights may still encode licensed or sensitive source information.
