# Research-information-system verification workflow

## Summary

Institutional research information systems should expose publication records, matches, and calculations so evaluated researchers can verify and correct them before assessment.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: CRIS record, researcher profile, output match, correction, provider workflow, or evaluation dossier.
- Typical representation: researcher-facing verification and correction workflow.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: assessed-party record review before metric evaluation.
- Empirical signature: researchers can inspect and correct records and calculations before they are used in evaluation..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Implements [evaluated-party data verification](../validations/evaluated_party_data_verification.md).
- Connects [commercial metric-provider accountability](../validations/commercial_metric_provider_accountability.md) to procurement.
- Improves provenance for paper-author-affiliation linkages.

## Operationalization

- Add researcher-facing verification portals.
- Log corrections and provenance.
- Use verification support as a procurement criterion for institutional systems and metric providers.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Hicks et al. (2015) recommends that researchers verify outputs and that universities implement this through research information systems and provider selection.

## Caveats

- Self-verification can introduce strategic corrections unless paired with audit rules.
- Researchers with less administrative support may use verification systems less effectively.
