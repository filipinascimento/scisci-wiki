# External oral-defense review

## Summary

High-touch review can combine a concise dossier with an oral defense before external elite reviewers.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: renewal review, oral defense, external reviewer, dossier, investigator, or feedback process.
- Typical representation: review-process design with live defense, external panel, and portfolio evidence.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: high-touch selection and renewal review for person-level funding.
- Empirical signature: investigators face detailed external critique through an oral defense rather than only written project scoring..

## Operationalization

- Identify renewal systems using live oral defense, external panel composition, dossier size, and feedback depth.
- Separate review as selection from review as feedback or mentoring.
- Track whether oral-defense review is associated with exploration, continuation, or program exit.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Azoulay et al. (2009) describes HHMI renewal as culminating in an oral defense before a high-caliber panel whose members are not HHMI researchers.
- The motif adds a concrete high-touch review format to funding-program design.

## Caveats

- Oral-defense effects are hard to distinguish from prestige, selection, and resource intensity.
- High-touch review may not scale to large public-agency programs.
