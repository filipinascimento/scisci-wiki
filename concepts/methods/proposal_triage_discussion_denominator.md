# Proposal-triage discussion denominator

## Summary

Whether an application reaches full panel discussion is itself a selected denominator after initial reviewer screening.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: grant application, triage decision, discussion flag, assigned-reviewer score, panel denominator, or final score.
- Typical representation: proposal flow table distinguishing triaged applications from discussed applications.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: selection denominator in grant peer-review designs.
- Empirical signature: a large share of applications is rejected without full panel discussion, so final discussed scores are conditional on a prior screen..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines peer-review denominators by linking it to [nih study section review process](nih_study_section_review_process.md) and [funded only peer review validation](../validations/funded_only_peer_review_validation.md).
- Useful as a reusable distinction when [rejected proposal line attrition](../mechanisms/rejected_proposal_line_attrition.md) is analyzed in science-of-science data.
- Adds cross-links to [proposal abundance evaluation bottleneck](../mechanisms/proposal_abundance_evaluation_bottleneck.md) so the motif is not read as an isolated result.

## Operationalization

- Code proposals as triaged without discussion versus discussed by the full study section.
- Estimate review-score relationships separately for initial-triage and full-discussion denominators when data allow.
- State whether an analysis covers all submitted proposals, discussed proposals, scored proposals, or funded proposals.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Li and Agha (2015) reports that roughly 40 to 50 percent of applications are triaged on initial reviewer scores and rejected without full discussion.
- This makes discussion status an important denominator before interpreting panel scores.

## Caveats

- Li and Agha's main outcome design uses funded R01s, so triaged unsuccessful applications are outside the main analytic sample.
- Triage may interact with applicant status, topic novelty, and reviewer expertise.
