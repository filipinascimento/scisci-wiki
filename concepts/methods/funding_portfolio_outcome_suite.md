# Funding portfolio outcome suite

## Summary

Funding portfolio outcome suite combines citation indicators with broader outputs and outcomes, such as innovation, clinical outcomes, software, patents, and economic activity, when evaluating research funding.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: funding portfolio, grant program, paper set, research output, outcome class, or evaluation report.
- Typical representation: multi-outcome dashboard, portfolio assessment suite, output taxonomy, or mixed quantitative-qualitative evaluation.
- Method target: evaluate funding outcomes with evidence streams aligned to the funder's mission rather than one citation metric.
- Empirical signature: citation influence is reported alongside translational, technical, economic, clinical, or societal outputs.

## Operationalization

- Define the funder's outcome goals, such as health advances, innovation, knowledge diffusion, or economic activity.
- Choose citation indicators for scholarly influence, not as a sole outcome measure.
- Add outcome measures for clinical progress, patents, software, datasets, policy use, or economic impact where relevant.
- Include expert interpretation and mission-specific context.
- Report uncertainty, time lags, and limitations for each outcome class.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Hutchins et al. (2016) argues that public interest in research outcomes is increasing and that funding entities need portfolio-level outcome evidence beyond success stories.
- The authors state that progress in solving scientific challenges, rather than citation counts, is the primary interest of funding agencies.
- They argue that comprehensive evaluation should incorporate many outputs and outcomes, including innovation, clinical outcomes, new software, patents, and economic activity.
- They frame RCR as one of several metrics that can assist decision makers when used judiciously with expert opinion.

## Caveats

- Outcome suites can become burdensome or inconsistent if every output class is required for every program.
- Long time lags make some outputs hard to attribute to specific grants.
- Broad suites still need safeguards against metric gaming and mission drift.
