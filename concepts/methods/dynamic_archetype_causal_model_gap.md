# Dynamic archetype causal model gap

## Summary

Static stage-to-stage associations leave open the need for dynamic and causal models of contribution-role transitions and career advancement.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: author, career stage, contribution archetype, transition, causal model, counterfactual, or advancement outcome.
- Typical representation: dynamic transition model or causal graph over contribution archetypes.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: causal interpretation gap in contribution-career trajectories.
- Empirical signature: descriptive archetype transitions cannot identify advancement effects without dynamic or counterfactual modeling.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines contribution-career causal methods by linking it to [contribution archetype career progression](../mechanisms/contribution_archetype_career_progression.md) and [bayesian contributorship imputation](bayesian_contributorship_imputation.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [matched career counterfactuals](matched_career_counterfactuals.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [descriptive association causal force gap](../validations/descriptive_association_causal_force_gap.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- Model archetype assignment with previous-stage roles, gender, position, productivity, and field histories.
- Use dynamic Bayesian networks, counterfactual analysis, or g-computation where data support them.
- Compare descriptive transition matrices with causal estimands before making career-advancement claims.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified local full text from Robinson-Garcia et al. (2020) says dynamic Bayesian networks, causal inference, counterfactual analysis, and g-computation would be needed for stronger claims about career advancement.
- The motif records a methodological gap following from the paper's descriptive design.

## Caveats

- Dynamic causal modeling requires longer and broader contribution data than the seven-year PLOS seed provides.
- Unmeasured mentorship, funding, and institutional status can still confound transitions.
