# OpenAlex open-infrastructure governance

## Summary

OpenAlex open-infrastructure governance is the combination of open data, open API, open-source code, nonprofit stewardship, and open scholarly infrastructure principles that makes a scholarly index inspectable and reusable.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: data license/access route, code repository, API policy, governance model, infrastructure principle, or sustainability commitment.
- Typical representation: openness checklist with data, code, API, cost, registration, governance, and sustainability fields.
- Method target: make scholarly infrastructure usable for transparent research evaluation and reproducible science-of-science workflows.
- Empirical signature: users can inspect, download, query, and reuse the infrastructure without toll-access barriers.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Provides governance context for using [OpenAlex](../datasets/openalex.md) as an open alternative to toll-access scholarly data.
- Connects [OpenAlex distribution channels](openalex_distribution_channels.md) to [responsible metrics](../measures/responsible_metrics.md), [transparent metric construction](transparent_metric_construction.md), and [bibliometric data access modes](bibliometric_data_access_modes.md).
- Helps distinguish open infrastructure from open access to individual papers: the data system itself is inspectable.

## Operationalization

- Record whether data, API, code, and documentation are open and under what terms.
- Prefer reproducible local dumps or scripted API access over non-exportable web-only workflows.
- Document governance and sustainability assumptions when a metric or dashboard depends on a live external service.
- Compare open infrastructure outputs against proprietary sources when source coverage or evaluation stakes are high.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Priem, Piwowar, and Orr (2022) frames OpenAlex as 100% open data, open API, and open-source code.
- The paper states that OpenAlex access routes are free and require no registration or permission.
- Priem et al. identify OpenAlex as an OurResearch nonprofit project and an early adopter of the Principles of Open Scholarly Infrastructure.
- The same full text argues that OpenAlex can improve transparency in research evaluation, navigation, representation, and discovery.

## Caveats

- Openness does not guarantee completeness, accuracy, or stable fields.
- Free access can still have practical limits for high-volume or production pipelines.
- Governance claims should be paired with current documentation and snapshot-specific provenance when used for evaluation.
