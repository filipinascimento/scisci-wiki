# Frontier-neutral disease solicitation

## Summary

Frontier-neutral disease solicitation is a proposal-call design that frames scientific opportunity around a problem domain rather than around existing literatures, pathways, or preferred mechanisms.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: proposal call, disease area, research problem, eligible hypothesis, or funding competition.
- Typical representation: broad problem-domain solicitation with minimal constraints on methods, mechanisms, or disciplinary route.
- Method target: invite variation in approaches so reviewer distance, proposal novelty, and frontier position can be observed.
- Empirical signature: proposals span diagnosis, treatment, care, prevention, mechanisms, and related frontier hypotheses rather than a single predefined research path.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Creates a clean proposal pool for [randomized reviewer-proposal assignment](randomized_reviewer_proposal_assignment.md).
- Supports [knowledge-frontier envelope](../representations/knowledge_frontier_envelope.md) studies by letting proposals occupy many locations in a problem-defined frontier.
- Pairs with [single-author proposal standardization](single_author_proposal_standardization.md) when applicant and proposal attributes need to be measured separately.
- Gives [biomedical frontier-review experiment](../datasets/biomedical_frontier_review_experiment.md) its problem-domain sampling frame.

## Operationalization

- Define the call around a broad scientific or clinical goal.
- Avoid prescribing a narrow research pathway, method, or literature.
- Allow proposals at different distances from existing expertise and paradigms.
- Record how eligibility rules shape the observed novelty distribution.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Boudreau et al. (2016) reports that the grant process was built around endocrine-related disease rather than a fixed literature or pathway.
- The first-stage call solicited approaches, ideas, and hypotheses aimed at progress in disease research, treatment, and care.
- This broad framing made proposal distance and novelty observable within a controlled review process rather than fixing the intellectual frontier in advance.

## Caveats

- Broad solicitations can increase proposal heterogeneity enough to make comparison difficult.
- Disease or problem framing is still a boundary; it can exclude relevant adjacent work.
- Reviewer pools must be broad enough to cover the proposal space created by the solicitation.
