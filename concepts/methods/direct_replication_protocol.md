# Direct replication protocol

## Summary

A direct replication protocol is a pre-specified workflow for recreating the conditions believed sufficient to produce a previously reported finding, then testing the same focal effect with new data.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: replication attempt, original study, focal effect, protocol, analysis plan, or audit record.
- Typical representation: protocol checklist, registered analysis plan, original-materials packet, replication report, or evidence table.
- Mechanism or measurement target: fidelity between original and replication conditions plus transparent separation of confirmatory analysis from post hoc interpretation.
- Empirical signature: original-author review, public registration, pre-selected key effect, archived materials and data, and independent audit of protocol completion.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Turns [replication and reproducibility](../validations/replication_reproducibility.md) into an executable research design.
- Gives [open science interventions](../validations/open_science_interventions.md) a concrete workflow unit that can be adopted by journals, funders, consortia, or labs.
- Supports [preregistration and registered reports](../validations/preregistration_registered_reports.md) by making the planned confirmatory test observable before data collection.
- Provides a quality layer for [replication success indicator suites](../measures/replication_success_indicator_suite.md), because success rates are interpretable only when fidelity and reporting are documented.
- Can be paired with [rolling replication article-pool assignment](rolling_replication_article_pool_assignment.md) before study selection when many teams select studies from a shared frame.
- Requires [focal-effect replication selection](focal_effect_replication_selection.md), [replication analysis reproduction audits](replication_analysis_reproduction_audit.md), and [replication effect harmonization](replication_effect_harmonization.md) when replications are aggregated across heterogeneous studies.
- Splits additional design and interpretation layers into [original-author fidelity review](original_author_fidelity_review.md), [replication moderator battery](replication_moderator_battery.md), [subdiscipline replication stratification](../validations/subdiscipline_replication_stratification.md), [confirmatory all-results replication reporting](../validations/confirmatory_all_results_replication_reporting.md), and [single replication truth-status boundary](../validations/single_replication_truth_status_boundary.md).
- Depends on a [replication outcome master dataset](../datasets/replication_outcome_master_dataset.md) and denominator accounting through [replication denominator scope limit](../validations/replication_denominator_scope_limit.md).

## Operationalization

- Select the original article, study, and focal effect before replication data collection.
- Retrieve original materials when possible and invite original authors to review the design.
- Register the replication protocol and analysis plan publicly before observing replication outcomes.
- Conduct the study, report all confirmatory results, archive materials and data, and audit the report for protocol adherence.
- Use [replication feasibility attrition](../validations/replication_feasibility_attrition.md) logs to distinguish protocol quality from selection into completed replications.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Open Science Collaboration (2015) describes a protocol that selected the study and key effect, contacted original authors for materials, prepared a design and analysis plan, obtained original-author and internal review, publicly registered the protocol, conducted the replication, wrote a final report, and audited the process.
- The same full text reports 100 completed replications by 270 contributing authors, with materials and data archived publicly on OSF to support transparency and later reuse.
- The protocol makes failures more interpretable because it documents whether the replication changed materials, sample, setting, procedure, or analysis from the original design.
- OSC also independently reproduced every replication analysis in a standardized R workflow, making the replication project's own computations auditable.
- A later OSC pass separates original-author fidelity review, moderator-battery construction, subdiscipline stratification, all-results confirmatory reporting, and the single-replication truth-status boundary from the broader protocol.

## Caveats

- A high-fidelity direct replication tests reliability more than theoretical explanation.
- Original-author review can improve fidelity but does not eliminate unknown moderators.
- Direct replication protocols are easier for low-cost laboratory or survey designs than for historical, rare-sample, expensive, or highly contextual studies.
