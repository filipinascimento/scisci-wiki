# Stratified-distance evaluator recruitment

## Summary

Stratified-distance evaluator recruitment is a design method that deliberately recruits evaluators across predefined intellectual-distance strata so proposal review contains analyzable variation in expertise proximity.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: evaluator, proposal area, recruitment stratum, seniority group, or evaluator-proposal pair.
- Typical representation: evaluator pool stratified by disease-area publication links, indirect links, no-link status, and seniority.
- Method target: create distance variation before random assignment rather than relying only on incidental reviewer heterogeneity.
- Empirical signature: the evaluator pool spans near and far expertise positions while still meeting eligibility and quality thresholds.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Prepares the design layer for [randomized reviewer-proposal assignment](randomized_reviewer_proposal_assignment.md).
- Makes [reviewer-proposal intellectual distance](../measures/reviewer_proposal_intellectual_distance.md) estimable with adequate support across the distance range.
- Complements [triple-blind proposal review](triple_blind_proposal_review.md) by shaping the evaluator pool before blinding and assignment.
- Helps avoid a review experiment where all evaluators cluster at the same expertise distance.

## Operationalization

- Define strata based on direct publication links, related-domain links, or no prior links to the focal disease or topic area.
- Cross distance strata with seniority, rank, or other evaluator-quality criteria.
- Recruit enough evaluators in each stratum to support randomized assignment and balance.
- Confirm that recruited evaluators meet conflict, availability, and competence constraints.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Boudreau et al. (2016) describes recruiting evaluators across disease-publishing, disease-linked, and no-link strata.
- The recruitment design also accounted for seniority, creating distance variation without treating far evaluators as low-quality reviewers.
- This stratification underlies the randomized 142-evaluator, 150-proposal review experiment.

## Caveats

- Recruitment strata are coarse and may not equal measured intellectual distance after proposal coding.
- Farther evaluators can differ systematically in motivation, review style, or availability.
- Stratification improves support but does not by itself identify effects without randomized assignment or careful controls.
