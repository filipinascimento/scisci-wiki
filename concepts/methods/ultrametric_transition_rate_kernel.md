# Ultrametric transition-rate kernel

## Summary

The ultradiffusive citation-decay model assumes transition rates between citation states decrease exponentially with ultrametric event distance.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: citation event, ultrametric distance, transition rate, state, post-peak trajectory, or decay model.
- Typical representation: distance-decay kernel over ultrametric citation-event states.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: mechanistic assumption inside ultradiffusive citation decay.
- Empirical signature: events farther apart in ultrametric time have lower transition rates between citation states..

## Operationalization

- Define event distance in ultrametric time space.
- Model transition rates between citation states as decreasing exponentially with that distance.
- State the kernel separately from empirical fit comparisons.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Parolo et al. (2015) defines ultrametric distance between citation events and assumes transition rates decay exponentially with that distance.
- The motif isolates a modeling assumption that is otherwise hidden inside the ultradiffusive formulation.

## Caveats

- The kernel is a modeling assumption rather than directly observed data.
- Alternative kernels could fit similar aggregate decay curves.
