# Domain-specific SciSci studies

## Summary

Domain-specific SciSci studies adapt science-of-science models, data sources, measures, and interventions to the culture, practices, questions, and evidence constraints of a particular field or country.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: discipline, specialty, country, institution type, data domain, policy setting, or field-specific corpus.
- Typical representation: domain-tuned model, field-specific validation design, local dataset, or policy-specific case study.
- Mechanism or measurement target: when general science-of-science regularities transfer, fail, or require local adaptation.
- Empirical signature: model performance, measurement validity, or policy relevance changes across fields, countries, or scientific cultures.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Provides a bridge from universal motifs to practical policy design.
- Flags where [responsible metrics](../measures/responsible_metrics.md), [field classifications](../measures/field_classifications.md), and [reference set construction](reference_set_construction.md) need field-specific choices.
- Uses [mission-aligned indicator selection](mission_aligned_indicator_selection.md) and [field-specific indicator suites](field_specific_indicator_suites.md) when domain differences are evaluation-relevant.
- Connects general [science of science framework](science_of_science_framework.md) pages to domain corpora, specialist validation, and local interventions.
- Helps decide when a wiki concept should include subpages for biomedical science, physics, social science, humanities, patents, countries, or funding systems.

## Operationalization

- Start from a general SciSci motif and state which domain-specific assumptions may fail.
- Build or select domain-relevant corpora, entity-resolution rules, field taxonomies, outcome measures, and validation labels.
- Compare domain-tuned results against cross-domain baselines and document transfer failures.
- Use local experts, field histories, and policy context to interpret whether measured regularities are actionable.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Fortunato et al. (2018) argues that SciSci seeks universal and domain-specific laws underlying science.
- The review warns that disciplinary differences in culture, habits, preferences, data, questions, and skills make some cross-domain insights difficult to apply and science policies difficult to implement.
- Fortunato et al. identify domain-specific SciSci studies as a route to model and identify opportunities adapted to the needs of individual research fields.
- The same review frames this as an outlook requirement, not a minor caveat: policy relevance depends on understanding when general mechanisms survive local context.

## Caveats

- Domain-specific adaptation can become overfitting if comparisons to broader baselines disappear.
- Local policy relevance does not guarantee causal identification.
- Field labels and country boundaries are themselves data products that can change over time.
