# Original-author fidelity review

## Summary

Original-author fidelity review is a replication-design method in which original authors provide materials and review the replication protocol before data collection.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: replication protocol, original study, original-author response, materials packet, or fidelity audit.
- Typical representation: author-contact log, materials receipt, reviewed protocol, deviation table, and public registration record.
- Method target: reduce avoidable divergence between an original study and a direct replication.
- Empirical signature: replication reports distinguish known design changes from unanticipated moderators or implementation failures.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Splits a fidelity layer out of [direct replication protocol](direct_replication_protocol.md).
- Provides a design check for [focal-effect replication selection](focal_effect_replication_selection.md).
- Complements [replication analysis reproduction audit](replication_analysis_reproduction_audit.md), which verifies computations after the replication is run.
- Supports [open science interventions](../validations/open_science_interventions.md) by making original materials and protocol review observable.

## Operationalization

- Contact original authors before finalizing the replication design.
- Request original materials, instructions, stimuli, code, and contextual implementation details.
- Ask original authors and internal reviewers to inspect the protocol and analysis plan.
- Register the reviewed protocol publicly before replication outcomes are known.
- Record unresolved design differences so later interpretation does not treat every failure as evidence against the original claim.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Open Science Collaboration (2015) states that replication teams contacted original authors, obtained materials, prepared protocols and analysis plans, secured original-author and internal review, registered protocols, and audited the final reports.
- The paper reports that the 100 completed replications used original materials and advance methodological review to maintain high fidelity to original designs.
- OSC still cautions that unanticipated differences in sample, setting, or procedure can alter observed effect magnitudes.

## Caveats

- Original-author review improves fidelity but can also introduce negotiation over what counts as the correct implementation.
- Some original materials may be unavailable, undocumented, or unusable in a new sample or setting.
- Fidelity review does not establish theoretical truth; it only narrows avoidable design divergence.
