# Replication archive checklist gate

## Summary

Large replication projects can require a complete materials, protocol, data, analysis, and review archive before a replication attempt enters the final evidence set.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: replication study, archive item, protocol, data file, analysis script, or completion checklist.
- Typical representation: checklist gate over protocol, materials, data, analysis, review, and public archive fields.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: replication evidence completeness before synthesis.
- Empirical signature: replication attempts are only finalized when required archival elements and analysis reproduction checks are complete.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Connects replication project governance to [direct replication protocol](direct_replication_protocol.md) and [replication analysis reproduction audit](replication_analysis_reproduction_audit.md).

## Operationalization

- Create a per-study checklist covering protocol, materials, sampling, exclusions, data, analysis code, results, and review status.
- Block final inclusion or mark incomplete items until the archive passes the checklist.
- Publish the checklist status alongside replication outcome data.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Open Science Collaboration (2015) describes public replication materials, protocols, data, analysis scripts, and project-level review workflows.
- The paper's supplementary workflow makes archive completeness a reusable gate for replication evidence.

## Caveats

- A complete archive does not guarantee a faithful or successful replication.
- Checklist gates can favor simpler studies whose materials are easier to archive.
