# Replication moderator battery

## Summary

Replication moderator battery is a structured set of original-study and replication-study features used to explore predictors of replication success.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: original-replication pair, study feature, replication feature, moderator index, or outcome indicator.
- Typical representation: standardized covariates, aggregate moderator indices, individual moderator variables, and correlation table.
- Method target: separate reproducibility predictors from uncontrolled post hoc explanations.
- Empirical signature: replication success indicators are tested against a predeclared or transparently reported moderator battery rather than one-off narratives.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Provides the measurement scaffold behind [original-evidence reproducibility gradient](../mechanisms/original_evidence_reproducibility_gradient.md).
- Helps interpret [replication success indicator suite](../measures/replication_success_indicator_suite.md) across multiple outcomes.
- Connects replication evidence to broader [scientometric measure construct validation](../validations/scientometric_measure_construct_validation.md).
- Gives [replication power expectation gap](../validations/replication_power_expectation_gap.md) a place among other candidate predictors.

## Operationalization

- Define candidate original-study features, such as original effect size, original p value, sample size, journal, effect importance, surprisingness, and author expertise.
- Define candidate replication features, such as replication power, sample size, implementation challenge, team expertise, and self-rated quality.
- Standardize and aggregate related variables when many-test false-positive risk is high.
- Correlate moderator indices and individual variables with multiple replication-success indicators.
- Report exploratory status and avoid treating moderator correlations as causal explanations.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Open Science Collaboration (2015) describes a moderator battery covering original-study and replication-study characteristics.
- OSC aggregated some variables into standardized indices to reduce false positives from many tests.
- The paper reports that original evidence strength was more consistently related to replication success than team expertise, quality, or implementation challenge.

## Caveats

- Moderator batteries are exploratory unless independently preregistered or externally replicated.
- Many correlated moderators can create unstable rankings of explanatory importance.
- The battery can miss unmeasured moderators in samples, settings, procedures, instruments, or theory-specific boundary conditions.
