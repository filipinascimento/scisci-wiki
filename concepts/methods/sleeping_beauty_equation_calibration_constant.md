# Sleeping-beauty equation calibration constant

## Summary

A multiplicative calibration constant is needed to turn van Raan's proportional sleeping-beauty equation into predicted counts.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: sleep length, sleep depth, wake intensity, count model, calibration constant, or sleeping-beauty count.
- Typical representation: scaled power-law count model with fitted multiplicative constant.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: calibration requirement for sleeping-beauty count prediction.
- Empirical signature: the proportional equation needs a constant before it predicts observed numbers of sleeping beauties..

## Operationalization

- Fit or report the multiplicative constant for the count relation over sleep time, sleep intensity, and wake intensity.
- Treat the equation as a scaling relation when the constant and fitting details are absent.
- Validate calibrated counts against held-out threshold cells where possible.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) says the equation gives counts after a constant factor is determined, while reporting dependencies on sleep time, sleep intensity, and wake intensity.
- The motif prevents users from treating an uncalibrated proportionality as a fully specified prediction model.

## Caveats

- Without the constant and fitting details, the equation is best treated as a scaling relation.
- Sparse tail cells make calibration unstable in the most extreme region.
