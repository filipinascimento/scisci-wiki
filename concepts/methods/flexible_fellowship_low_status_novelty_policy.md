# Flexible-fellowship low-status novelty policy

## Summary

Flexible person-level support can be used as a policy response for low-status researchers vulnerable to conformity pressure.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: graduate fellow, early-career researcher, fellowship, project assistantship, block support, or status group.
- Typical representation: policy-design rule targeting flexible support to low-status or early-career scientists.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: funding intervention for novelty under status-contingent pressure.
- Empirical signature: low-status researchers may benefit from flexible support that is not tied to a specific project or PI grant..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines status-sensitive funding design by linking it to [block funding novelty buffer](../mechanisms/block_funding_novelty_buffer.md) and [funding autonomy exploration](../mechanisms/funding_autonomy_exploration.md).
- Useful as a reusable distinction when [creative labor force novelty waste](../consequences/creative_labor_force_novelty_waste.md) is analyzed in science-of-science data.
- Adds cross-links to [status conformity pressure](../mechanisms/status_conformity_pressure.md) so the motif is not read as an isolated result.

## Operationalization

- Identify flexible fellowships, stable internal funds, or person-level awards not tied to a specific project.
- Target or stratify support by career stage, rank, institution status, and vulnerability to conformity pressure.
- Compare topic novelty under fellowship support versus project-bound assistantship support.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wang et al. (2018) connects the findings to arguments for more NSF graduate fellowships rather than project-bound research assistantships.
- The motif translates status-contingent findings into a policy-design hypothesis.

## Caveats

- This is a policy extrapolation, not a directly tested intervention in the paper.
- Flexible support can also reduce accountability if not paired with appropriate evaluation.
