# Indicator-suite pluralism

## Summary

Evaluation should use multiple indicators as a robustness and governance strategy when single metrics invite gaming, false precision, or construct reduction.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: indicator suite, metric profile, evaluation dossier, decision rule, or composite score.
- Typical representation: multi-indicator profile mapped to distinct constructs.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: plural evidence base for consequential evaluation decisions.
- Empirical signature: decisions are based on a separated profile of indicators rather than one universal score..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [metric pluralism and productive specialization](../mechanisms/metric_pluralism_productive_specialization.md).
- Adds a portfolio design to [responsible metrics](../measures/responsible_metrics.md).
- Counters [citation metric gaming](../validations/citation_metric_gaming.md) and false precision.

## Operationalization

- Require a multi-indicator profile for consequential decisions.
- Map each indicator to a distinct construct.
- Report dimensions separately before any composite and test redundancy and gaming incentives.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Hicks et al. (2015) recommends multiple indicators for a robust plural picture and warns that single indicators invite gaming and goal displacement.

## Caveats

- More indicators can multiply targets for gaming.
- A plural suite can collapse back into a single score if composited carelessly.
