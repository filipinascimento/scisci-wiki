# Open-access route citation heterogeneity

## Summary

The OA citation advantage is not uniform; Green, Hybrid, Bronze, and Gold show different normalized citation patterns.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: article, OA route, field-year cohort, or citation outcome.
- Typical representation: field-year normalized citation outcome by OA route.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: heterogeneity in citation associations across OA routes.
- Empirical signature: OA route categories differ in normalized citation impact after accounting for age and discipline..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines [open access citation advantage](../mechanisms/open_access_citation_advantage.md) by route.
- Useful for separating Green, Hybrid, Bronze, and Gold citation patterns.
- Connects route labels to [field normalized citation impact](field_normalized_citation_impact.md).

## Operationalization

- Estimate field-year normalized citation outcomes by OA route with route-by-year and route-by-field interactions.
- Report route assignment uncertainty and route precedence rules.
- Pair route effects with selection-bias checks before causal interpretation.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Piwowar et al. (2018) reports OA overall above expected citations, with Green, Hybrid, and Bronze above average and Gold below average in the WoS citation analysis.
- This supports route-level heterogeneity rather than one OA effect.

## Caveats

- Route assignment is observational and entangled with journal prestige, author selection, and funder effects.
- Gold OA outcomes can vary strongly by journal age and indexing history.
