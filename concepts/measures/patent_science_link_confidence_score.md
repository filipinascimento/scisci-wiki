# Patent-science link confidence score

## Summary

Patent-science link confidence score grades an inferred patent-to-paper citation by how strongly the patent reference string matches structured publication metadata.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: patent-reference-to-paper candidate, confidence level, paper metadata field, or released patent-paper edge.
- Typical representation: ordinal confidence score, thresholded link table, or score-specific precision/recall profile.
- Measure target: expose uncertainty in [unstructured patent-reference matching](../methods/unstructured_patent_reference_matching.md) rather than releasing a binary match only.
- Empirical signature: users can raise or lower a score cutoff depending on their tolerance for false positives and false negatives.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Makes [Reliance on Science dataset](../datasets/reliance_on_science_dataset.md) links tunable for different research designs.
- Supports conservative lower-bound analyses of [patent-paper links](../datasets/patent_paper_links.md) and broader exploratory analyses of [scientific non-patent references](../datasets/scientific_non_patent_references.md).
- Feeds [patent-science precision-recall calibration](../validations/patent_science_precision_recall_calibration.md) by attaching empirical validation evidence to each threshold.
- Is generated by [patent-reference metadata evidence scoring](../methods/patent_reference_metadata_evidence_scoring.md) and interpreted through [patent-science threshold design tradeoff](../validations/patent_science_threshold_design_tradeoff.md).
- Helps downstream users avoid treating all [patent citation-to-science link table](../datasets/patent_citation_to_science_link_table.md) rows as equally reliable.

## Operationalization

- Assign a score using evidence from author-name match, title similarity, journal name, volume, issue, pages, and metadata conflicts.
- Penalize common surnames, misleading short terms, mismatching initials, and conflicting page or volume evidence.
- Break candidate ties using overall confidence, then title score and volume/issue/page score.
- Release confidence with every patent-paper edge and document the recommended cutoff.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Marx and Fuegi (2020) releases PCS links with confidence scores from 1 to 10.
- The paper excludes confidence scores 1 and 2 from the distributed link file because validation shows very few correct matches at those levels.
- Appendix documentation states that even score 3 contains many incorrect matches and that many users will prefer score 4 or higher.
- Confidence scores are validated empirically by manual precision samples and known-good recall samples, not only by internal scoring logic.

## Caveats

- Confidence scores are not probabilities unless calibrated for a particular corpus and threshold.
- Score meaning can change when the paper corpus changes from MAG to OpenAlex, Dimensions, PubMed, or Crossref.
- A high-confidence link still needs source-role and jurisdiction caveats before being interpreted as inventor knowledge use.
