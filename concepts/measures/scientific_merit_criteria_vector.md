# Scientific-merit criteria vector

## Summary

Proposal review can be represented as a multi-criterion scientific-merit vector rather than as a single prestige signal.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: grant application, review criterion, study-section score, proposal feature, or reviewer instruction.
- Typical representation: criteria vector over significance, approach, innovation, investigator, and environment.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: measurement basis for NIH scientific-merit scoring.
- Empirical signature: formal review criteria define multiple evaluative dimensions before they collapse into a score..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines peer-review measurement by linking it to [nih study section review process](../methods/nih_study_section_review_process.md) and [nih peer review percentile score](nih_peer_review_percentile_score.md).
- Useful as a reusable distinction when [grant peer review value added](../validations/grant_peer_review_value_added.md) is analyzed in science-of-science data.
- Adds cross-links to [applicant prestige residualization stack](../validations/applicant_prestige_residualization_stack.md) so the motif is not read as an isolated result.

## Operationalization

- Extract or code review criteria such as significance, approach, innovation, investigator qualifications, and environment.
- Use the criteria vector to distinguish formal scientific merit from applicant prestige, budget, or institute priorities.
- Compare criterion salience across review regimes when panel text, critiques, or instructions are available.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Li and Agha (2015) says NIH reviewers were instructed to assess scientific and technical merit using five explicit criteria.
- The motif makes the review score's formal input dimensions visible before residualizing applicant prestige.

## Caveats

- Formal criteria do not prove that reviewers weight the criteria as instructed.
- A collapsed percentile score hides which criterion drove the final panel evaluation.
