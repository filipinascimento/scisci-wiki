# Pre-death subfield momentum split

## Summary

Pre-death subfield momentum split stratifies star-linked fields by their recent pre-event activity to test whether dormant, cooling, or active fields respond differently to a leadership shock.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: subfield, field-year, star-linked research area, recent publication share, or event-study stratum.
- Typical representation: high versus low recent-activity split, five-year activity share, field-momentum bin, or treatment-effect heterogeneity table.
- Measurement target: pre-shock field momentum and its relationship to post-shock entry.
- Empirical signature: outsider entry differs between subfields with high recent activity and those with lower pre-death momentum.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds a field-life-cycle layer to [star-scientist entry deterrence](../mechanisms/star_scientist_entry_deterrence.md).
- Complements [average publication-year hot-area detection](average_publication_year_hot_area_detection.md) by measuring pre-event momentum rather than cluster age.
- Helps test whether post-star entry reflects opportunity in fading fields, active frontiers, or mature blocked areas.
- Supports external checks against [field saturation projection](../validations/field_saturation_projection.md).

## Operationalization

- Define each treated subfield using an outcome-independent delineation method.
- Compute the share of subfield output occurring in the recent pre-event window, such as five years before death.
- Split subfields into high- and low-momentum strata or use the measure continuously.
- Estimate post-event collaborator and non-collaborator outcomes separately by momentum stratum.
- Compare with controls assigned the same counterfactual event year.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Azoulay, Fons-Rosen, and Graff Zivin (2019) uses pre-death activity shares to test heterogeneity in post-star entry.
- The paper asks whether fields that were relatively quiet or active before the death respond differently to the removal of a star.
- Its appendix places this split alongside intellectual, social, and resource-barrier measures, making pre-event field momentum a distinct conditioning variable.
- This split helps avoid treating all star-linked subfields as equally mature or equally open to entry.

## Caveats

- Low recent activity can mean exhaustion, lack of opportunity, or temporary data sparsity.
- High recent activity can reflect the star's own productivity rather than field-wide momentum unless star-authored papers are handled carefully.
- Momentum splits can be sensitive to the chosen pre-event window.
- Publication volume does not measure latent ideas or unpublished work.
