# Doctorate duration as frontier-entry signal

## Summary

Doctoral age, doctoral duration, and postdoctoral prevalence can proxy for rising training demands before independent work.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: doctoral cohort, field, highest-degree age, time-to-doctorate, postdoc duration, or frontier-entry proxy.
- Typical representation: education-duration indicators linked to field and cohort.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: institutional training-time signal of knowledge-burden pressure.
- Empirical signature: doctoral and postdoctoral training durations rise as frontier entry requires more accumulated knowledge.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines knowledge-burden measurement by linking it to [delayed independent contribution window](../consequences/delayed_independent_contribution_window.md) and [doctoral coauthorship burden triangulation](../validations/doctoral_coauthorship_burden_triangulation.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [age at first invention](age_at_first_invention.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [human capital transfer bottleneck](../mechanisms/human_capital_transfer_bottleneck.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- Measure age at highest degree, time-to-doctorate, and postdoc incidence or duration by field and cohort.
- Compare these signals with age at first patent or first independent publication.
- Use Nobel laureate or elite cohort doctorate ages only as supplemental historical evidence.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Jones (2009) compares patent-based first invention age with age at highest degree and cites rising doctoral age, longer doctoral programs, more postdocs, and Nobel doctorate-age trends.
- The paper treats these education signals as external evidence of rising training burden.

## Caveats

- Doctoral training includes research, so it imperfectly separates learning from innovation.
- Institutional rules and labor-market conditions can also lengthen doctoral duration.
