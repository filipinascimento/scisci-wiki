# Zero-citation deep-sleep extreme

## Summary

A stricter extremity condition requires a sleeping beauty to receive no citations in every sleep year, not merely a low average citation rate.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, sleep year, annual citation count, sleep length, wake intensity, or extreme case.
- Typical representation: strict uncited-sleep indicator combined with high awakening intensity.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: extreme delayed-recognition severity.
- Empirical signature: an extreme sleeping beauty has zero citations throughout the sleep period and a high citation burst immediately afterward..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines sleeping-beauty severity measure by linking it to [sleep depth length](sleep_depth_length.md) and [awakening intensity](awakening_intensity.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [sleeping beauty population rarity](sleeping_beauty_population_rarity.md) is interpreted from citation histories.
- Adds cross-links to [sleeping beauty population rarity](sleeping_beauty_population_rarity.md) so delayed-recognition and ranking motifs stay connected.

## Operationalization

- Flag cases with a fixed sleep length and zero citations in every sleeping year.
- Pair the strict sleep indicator with a wake-intensity threshold such as cw greater than 60.
- Distinguish zero-citation sleep from average low-citation sleep.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) describes the Romans case as having the longest sleep, zero citations in all sleeping years, and wake intensity above 60.
- The motif gives a stricter extremity rule than average citations per year during sleep.

## Caveats

- Van Raan uses the stricter condition for the extreme case while the main deep-sleep bins allow up to one citation per year on average.
- A zero-citation rule is sensitive to database coverage and cited-reference errors.
