# Regional collaboration hubs

## Summary

Regional collaboration hubs are countries, cities, or institutions that connect peripheral local systems into broader international scientific collaboration networks.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: country, region, city, institution, or international collaboration graph.
- Typical representation: hub node, regional bridge, core-periphery connector, or high-degree/high-betweenness place.
- Mechanism or measurement target: regional entry points and decentralized routes into global science.
- Empirical signature: peripheral countries often connect to the international network through nearby or culturally linked hubs rather than directly to a single global center.

## Operationalization

- Build a country or city collaboration network from affiliation-derived coauthorships.
- Identify countries or places that connect regional neighbors to the larger collaboration graph.
- Use degree, weighted degree, betweenness, community membership, or core-periphery position to distinguish hubs from peripheral endpoints.
- Compare hub structure across years and fields.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wagner and Leydesdorff (2005) reports that the global country collaboration network became more decentralized between 1990 and 2000.
- The paper describes regional hubs emerging, including South Africa, and notes that smaller or peripheral countries were more likely to link to the international network through regional hubs than through an advanced country.
- Wagner and Leydesdorff also describe geographically proximate country-pair relationships and otherwise isolated regional groupings, making regional structure an explicit part of international collaboration.

## Caveats

- Hub status depends on the edge threshold and the chosen geographic scale.
- Regional hubs may reflect colonial ties, language, aid programs, proximity, field specialization, or database coverage rather than one mechanism.
- Country-level hubs can hide city-level or institution-level brokerage.
