# Publisher OA portfolio profile

## Summary

Publishers can be profiled by both OA volume and route composition, separating sheer scale from openness share.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: publisher, DOI cohort, OA route, publication year, or article count.
- Typical representation: publisher-level OA route shares, counts, and route entropy.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: publisher-level openness portfolio rather than one OA rate.
- Empirical signature: publishers with similar OA counts differ in route mix, and high-share publishers may differ from high-volume publishers..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [open access prevalence measurement](open_access_prevalence_measurement.md) to publisher portfolios.
- Useful for studying publisher strategy, route shifts, and market structure.
- Connects publisher-hosted route precedence to publisher-level route composition.

## Operationalization

- Normalize publisher names, imprints, mergers, and Crossref publisher metadata.
- Compute publisher-level route shares, counts, and route entropy for recent DOI cohorts.
- Compare publishers by both absolute OA volume and proportion open.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Piwowar et al. (2018) compares top publishers by number and proportion of OA articles and shows different profiles for Elsevier, PLOS, Hindawi, APS, and others.
- This supports a portfolio view of publisher OA behavior.

## Caveats

- Publisher names, imprints, mergers, and Crossref metadata require normalization.
- Publisher-level measures can hide journal-level route heterogeneity.
