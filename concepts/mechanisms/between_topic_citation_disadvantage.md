# Between-topic citation disadvantage

## Summary

Between-topic citation disadvantage is the mechanism by which demographic groups receive lower aggregate citation impact because they are overrepresented in topics that receive fewer citations on average.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: demographic group, topic, paper, field, citation outcome, or topic portfolio.
- Typical representation: group-topic overrepresentation matrix with topic citation density, decomposition of citation gaps, or topic-sorted citation curves.
- Mechanism: unequal access to high-prestige topics, social valuation of topics, funding disparities, and topic-level attention differences translate topic placement into citation gaps.
- Empirical signature: a group is concentrated in lower-cited topics and underrepresented in higher-cited topics.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Decomposes [intersectional topic stratification](intersectional_topic_stratification.md) into a topic-allocation component.
- Links [topic-identity homophily](topic_identity_homophily.md) to [attention inequality](attention_inequality.md).
- Warns that [field-normalized citation impact](../measures/field_normalized_citation_impact.md) may miss finer topic-level valuation differences.
- Gives [responsible metrics](../measures/responsible_metrics.md) a concrete demographic-topic audit target.
- Supports policy designs such as [topic-network equity intervention portfolio](../methods/topic_network_equity_intervention_portfolio.md), where undervalued topics receive resources.

## Operationalization

- Estimate topic-level citation density within a field, year, or topic model.
- Estimate each demographic group's topic distribution.
- Compare group representation in low-, medium-, and high-citation topics.
- Decompose group citation gaps into between-topic placement and within-topic citation differences.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Kozlowski et al. (2022) shows that citation gaps remain after broad field normalization and then analyzes topic-level citation density.
- The paper reports that average topic citations are positively correlated with the presence of Asian and White men in the analyzed Social Sciences and Health topic maps.
- Kozlowski et al. conclude that minoritized groups are overrepresented in lowly cited topics and underrepresented in highly cited topics.
- The full text further argues that differential topic citation rates and unequal demographic topic distributions jointly create negative effects for marginalized groups and for science.

## Caveats

- Lower citation density does not imply lower social, clinical, cultural, or intellectual value.
- Topic citation density can be shaped by field size, funding, publication venues, database coverage, and citation norms.
- Between-topic disadvantage should be separated from [within-topic citation disadvantage](within_topic_citation_disadvantage.md) before drawing policy conclusions.
- Funding underrecognized topics should not be treated as a substitute for access to high-prestige networks.
