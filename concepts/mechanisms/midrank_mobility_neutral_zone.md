# Midrank Mobility Neutral Zone

## Summary

Midrank mobility neutral zone is the empirical pattern in which scientists from medium-ranked institutions move without a detectable bias toward elite, middle, or lower-ranked destinations.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: institutional rank stratum, scientist move, origin-destination transition, or mobility null model.
- Typical representation: rank-transition cell pattern with random-like midrank behavior.
- Mechanism: stratification is concentrated in elite and lower-rank within-club movement, while midrank mobility is comparatively neutral.
- Empirical signature: transition probabilities for midrank origins are indistinguishable from a randomized baseline.

## Operationalization

- Group institutions into rank strata.
- Estimate observed transition likelihoods from midrank origins to destination strata.
- Compare observed transitions with a randomized or shuffled baseline.
- Report whether midrank moves show directional bias or random-like mixing.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Deville et al. (2014) reports that cross-moves between elite and lower-ranked institutions are underrepresented.
- In contrast, scientists from medium-ranked institutions move to the next institution with probabilities indistinguishable from the random case.
- The authors interpret those medium-rank movements as showing no bias toward middle, elite, or lower-ranked institutions.

## Caveats

- The neutral zone depends on rank binning and the chosen null model.
- Midrank neutrality in physics may not hold in fields with different labor markets or institutional hierarchies.
- The pattern is descriptive unless paired with selection and career-stage controls.
