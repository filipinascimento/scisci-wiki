# Experimental-to-analytic specialization shift

## Summary

Specialized research careers can shift their focal task from experiment performance toward data analysis by mid-career.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: author, career stage, contribution profile, PE role probability, AD role probability, or archetype.
- Typical representation: stage-by-role probability profile for specialized researchers.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: career-stage task migration inside specialized contribution profiles.
- Empirical signature: the AD-minus-PE differential grows across junior, early, and mid-career stages among specialized-profile researchers.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines contribution-career mechanism by linking it to [task specialization](task_specialization.md) and [contribution role vectors](../representations/contribution_role_vectors.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [career stage contributorship panel](../representations/career_stage_contributorship_panel.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [leader specialized supporting archetypes](../representations/leader_specialized_supporting_archetypes.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- Compute PE and AD median predicted role probabilities by author-stage among Specialized-profile researchers.
- Track the AD-minus-PE differential across junior, early, and mid-career stages.
- Compare the shift against leader and supporting archetypes to avoid conflating specialization with role breadth.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified local full text from Robinson-Garcia et al. (2020) describes Specialized profiles around PE and AD and notes a PE-to-AD shift by mid-career.
- The motif refines task specialization as a dynamic rather than fixed career profile.

## Caveats

- The result depends on predicted PLOS role labels, coarse career bins, and the five-role taxonomy.
- Field and team-size composition may influence which tasks are visible in statements.
