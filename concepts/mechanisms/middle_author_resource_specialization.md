# Middle-author resource specialization

## Summary

Middle authorship is associated with lower writing/design claims but stronger resource, reagent, tool, data, or material contribution claims.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: author-paper position, middle author, contribution task, career stage, archetype, or team size.
- Typical representation: author-position by contribution-role matrix with CT/resource emphasis.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: positional specialization in contribution statements.
- Empirical signature: middle authors show higher resource/tool/material contribution shares and lower writing/design shares than first authors.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines author-position contribution mechanism by linking it to [author order contribution proxy limits](../validations/author_order_contribution_proxy_limits.md) and [contribution role vectors](../representations/contribution_role_vectors.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [bibliometric contributorship predictor set](../representations/bibliometric_contributorship_predictor_set.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [gendered author position gap](../measures/gendered_author_position_gap.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- In papers with at least three authors, compare CT versus WR, CE, PE, and AD shares for middle authors relative to first and last authors.
- Stratify by career stage and archetype to distinguish positional abundance from role specialization.
- Check whether the pattern persists after team-size controls.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified local full text from Robinson-Garcia et al. (2020) reports that first authors are broad contributors while middle authors are less involved in writing/design and more associated with resources.
- The motif isolates a contribution niche inside the broad middle-author position.

## Caveats

- Middle authorship is mechanically common in large teams.
- CT is a broad mixed category and may merge several distinct kinds of support.
