# Communicated-contribution incorporation threshold

## Summary

Communicated-contribution incorporation threshold is the mechanism by which a discovery becomes a scientific contribution only after it is communicated, perceived, and incorporated into the common stock of knowledge.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: discovery, paper, communication, reader, field, or knowledge stock.
- Typical representation: discovery-to-communication-to-use pathway.
- Mechanism: uncommunicated or unnoticed work cannot fully enter collective knowledge even if the underlying discovery exists.
- Empirical signature: recognition and downstream use depend on whether the result reaches and is incorporated by relevant audiences.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Places [recognition-visibility coupling](recognition_visibility_coupling.md) inside a broader incorporation process.
- Links [multiple-discovery redundancy function](multiple_discovery_redundancy_function.md) to the communication status of each discovery.
- Connects [article readership reach rate](../measures/article_readership_reach_rate.md) to the social construction of a contribution.
- Helps distinguish discovery priority from contribution uptake.

## Operationalization

- Trace when a finding was made, communicated, noticed, cited, replicated, or used.
- Compare unpublished discoveries, low-visibility publications, and widely incorporated communications.
- Use citation contexts, textbook uptake, replication, and review mentions as incorporation indicators.
- Treat priority disputes as partly about communication and incorporation, not only first occurrence.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Merton (1968) argues that innovations must be communicated into the common fund of knowledge to count as scientific contributions.
- The claim links the reward system of science to the communication system of science.
- This motif is distinct from status-based visibility because it defines the threshold that status can help or hinder a contribution to cross.
- It also helps explain why multiple discoveries can be socially useful even when one priority claim is earlier.

## Caveats

- Incorporation is not identical to citation count; some contributions diffuse through methods, teaching, or tacit practice.
- Communication can be distorted by language, access, field boundaries, and status cues.
- The threshold can be unfair when high-quality work is blocked from visibility.
