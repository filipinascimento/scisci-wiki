# Failure phase-down option

## Summary

Soft nonrenewal and bridge periods make exploratory failure less catastrophic by preserving time to wind down or replace support.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: funding program, nonrenewal event, bridge period, laboratory, research staff, or transition support.
- Typical representation: funding-design option with phase-down duration and bridge-support fields.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: risk buffer in high-autonomy research funding.
- Empirical signature: programs with failure phase-down support reduce the immediate penalty of unsuccessful exploratory lines..

## Operationalization

- Code funding designs for post-termination bridge support, phase-down duration, and lab continuity rights.
- Measure whether personnel, equipment, and research lines can be preserved while replacement funding is sought.
- Compare exploratory output before and after nonrenewal risk becomes salient.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Azoulay et al. (2009) describes HHMI nonrenewal as including a two-year phase-down rather than abrupt termination.
- The motif isolates the failure-buffering component of person-level funding.

## Caveats

- The phase-down option is bundled with larger budgets, prestige, selection, and high-touch review.
- Bridge support may protect incumbent laboratories more than early-career entrants.
