# Publisher altmetric promotion loop

## Summary

Publisher altmetric promotion loop is the mechanism motif that publishers' social-media promotion and tracker displays can shape the same online attention metrics later used to evaluate articles.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: publisher post, article page, altmetric badge, tracker display, campaign, or attention event.
- Typical representation: promotional exposure, publisher social-media campaign, tracking-site visibility, or metric intervention loop.
- Mechanism target: explain how altmetric counts can be both outcome measures and targets of promotion.
- Empirical signature: publisher-controlled exposure precedes or amplifies online mentions counted by altmetric providers.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Connects [metric platform proliferation](metric_platform_proliferation.md) with [altmetric author self-mention bias](../validations/altmetric_author_self_mention_bias.md).
- Adds an exposure mechanism to [social media attention channels](../datasets/social_media_attention_channels.md).
- Supports interpretation of altmetrics as partly platform- and promotion-mediated traces.

## Operationalization

- Record publisher posts, press releases, badges, tracker placement, homepage features, and campaign timing.
- Model altmetric events before and after publisher exposure.
- Distinguish author-driven, publisher-driven, and third-party mentions where possible.
- Test whether promotion residuals explain source-specific attention.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Thelwall et al. (2013) describes publishers using social-media mentions and tracking sites to promote and monitor current articles.
- The paper motivates the loop but does not directly estimate publisher-promotion effects.

## Caveats

- Publisher promotion can reflect expected article importance rather than cause attention.
- Promotion records are often incomplete.
- The loop may differ strongly across publishers and platforms.
