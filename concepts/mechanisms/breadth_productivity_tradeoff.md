# Breadth-productivity tradeoff

## Summary

Broader expertise can raise creative productivity and reduce implementation dependence, but it becomes increasingly costly as knowledge depth rises.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: innovator, expertise breadth, invention rate, training cost, team dependence, or frontier depth.
- Typical representation: trade-off curve between breadth returns and breadth acquisition costs.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: why specialization and teamwork substitute for individual breadth.
- Empirical signature: broader expertise raises idea production or reduces team dependence only up to the point where training costs dominate.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines knowledge-burden mechanism by linking it to [education specialization substitution](education_specialization_substitution.md) and [knowledge breadth idea arrival function](../methods/knowledge_breadth_idea_arrival_function.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [minimum expertise team assembly](minimum_expertise_team_assembly.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [knowledge circle expertise arcs](../representations/knowledge_circle_expertise_arcs.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- Measure breadth proxies against invention rate and team dependence.
- Estimate productivity returns to field-spanning expertise net of training time or cost.
- Compare breadth returns across shallow and deep fields.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Jones (2009) argues that broader foundational knowledge improves combinatorial creativity, feasibility evaluation, and team-formation costs while deeper frontiers make breadth more expensive.
- This isolates the productivity-return side of education-specialization substitution.

## Caveats

- The motif is adjacent to education-specialization substitution and should be used for the productivity-return side of breadth.
- Breadth proxies may capture status, resources, or field position as well as expertise.
