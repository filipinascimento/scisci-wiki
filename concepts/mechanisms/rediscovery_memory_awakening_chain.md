# Rediscovery-memory awakening chain

## Summary

A two-step awakening mechanism links later rediscovery to remembered prior work and then to a citation connection.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: rediscovery event, colleague memory, citation link, sleeping paper, waking paper, or field readiness.
- Typical representation: narrative mechanism chain from rediscovery to citation awakening.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: social-memory pathway in delayed recognition.
- Empirical signature: a later rediscovery triggers memory of an old paper, establishing the citation link that awakens it..

## Operationalization

- Code an awakening chain as later rediscovery, colleague or local memory, citation link, and subsequent citation trajectory.
- Pair narrative evidence with citation timing and author comments when available.
- Compare memory-mediated awakenings with author-mediated and benchmark-mediated awakenings.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) reports that the later community was ready, the phenomenon was rediscovered, a colleague remembered Romans's paper, and the citation link was made.
- The motif captures social memory as a bridge between independent rediscovery and bibliometric awakening.

## Caveats

- The evidence is narrative and case-specific.
- Treat the chain as a mechanism hypothesis unless observed across more cases.
