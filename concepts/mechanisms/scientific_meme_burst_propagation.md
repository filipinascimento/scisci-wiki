# Scientific-Meme Burst Propagation

## Summary

Scientific-meme burst propagation is the mechanism by which words and phrases in scientific titles and abstracts diffuse through citation networks, sometimes rising in bursts around new paradigms.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: phrase, title term, abstract term, citation community, field, or paper cohort.
- Typical representation: temporal phrase-frequency trace overlaid on a citation network.
- Mechanism: lexical signals move through linked papers as communities adopt, reuse, and recombine emerging concepts.
- Empirical signature: fast growth in phrase use coincides with citation-community expansion or new paradigm formation.

## Operationalization

- Extract recurring phrases from titles and abstracts by year.
- Track burst timing, peak intensity, and decay within citation communities.
- Compare phrase bursts with new citation clusters, journals, venues, or funding programs.
- Validate bursts against expert histories of paradigms or field formation.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Fortunato et al. (2018) reports that title and abstract words or phrases propagate through citation networks.
- The review links such propagation to bursts that correspond to new paradigms.
- Fortunato et al. separate this diffusion dynamic from the slower linear expansion of the overall conceptual territory of science.

## Caveats

- Phrase bursts can reflect branding, indexing conventions, or fashionable labels rather than conceptual change.
- Synonymy and phrase drift make raw term counts fragile.
- Citation-network propagation should be distinguished from independent parallel adoption.
