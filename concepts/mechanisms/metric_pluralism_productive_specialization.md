# Metric pluralism and productive specialization

## Summary

Metric pluralism and productive specialization is the claim that broader indicator portfolios can recognize multiple valuable scientific roles rather than forcing all researchers into a single citation-count currency.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: researcher, team, institution, output, role, evaluation system, or indicator portfolio.
- Typical representation: multi-indicator profile, alternative-impact dashboard, role-aware evaluation, or plural success taxonomy.
- Mechanism: plural indicators can make different forms of scientific contribution visible and reduce pressure toward one narrow performance signal.
- Empirical signature: evaluation systems recognize publications, communication, teaching, peer support, data sharing, reviewing, societal use, and field-altering questions as distinct contribution dimensions.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds a mechanism layer to [responsible metrics](../measures/responsible_metrics.md): indicator portfolios should support multiple productive roles.
- Responds directly to [citation-count currency hierarchy](citation_count_currency_hierarchy.md), where one dominant citation signal compresses different scientific contributions into a single hierarchy.
- Connects alternative indicators, [altmetrics](../datasets/altmetrics.md), contribution roles, and peer-help measures to research evaluation.
- Provides a policy-facing counterpart to [task specialization](task_specialization.md) and [contribution role vectors](../representations/contribution_role_vectors.md).
- Helps avoid interpreting single citation metrics as complete measures of scientific value.

## Operationalization

- Define the dimensions of scientific contribution that matter for the evaluation context.
- Pair citation indicators with complementary evidence such as data sharing, software, teaching, peer review, communication, policy use, reproducibility, and role statements.
- Report each indicator separately before building composites.
- Audit whether plural indicators reduce or increase gaming, inequity, duplication, and target substitution.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Fortunato et al. (2018) argues that broadening performance indicators can improve science because citation-count currencies create hierarchical rich-get-richer dynamics.
- The review states that integrating citation metrics with alternative indicators can promote pluralism and create new dimensions of productive specialization.
- Fortunato et al. explicitly describe science as an ecosystem requiring publications, communicators, teachers, detail-oriented experts, novel question askers, and researchers who answer such questions.
- The review warns that indicator proliferation still requires models of what each indicator captures and how it may be misused.

## Caveats

- More indicators do not automatically make evaluation fairer; they can create more targets to game.
- Plural profiles require interpretation, mission alignment, and domain-specific context.
- Composite scores can erase the pluralism that motivated the indicator portfolio.
