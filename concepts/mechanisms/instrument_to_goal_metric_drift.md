# Instrument-to-goal metric drift

## Summary

Quantitative indicators become harmful when they drift from instruments for understanding research into goals that researchers and institutions optimize.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: indicator, policy goal, evaluation system, institution, researcher, or funding formula.
- Typical representation: proxy-to-target role drift pathway.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: goal displacement caused by metric optimization.
- Empirical signature: policy language and behavior shift from using a metric as evidence to treating metric improvement as the objective..

## Operationalization

- Audit policy language for score-as-goal framing.
- Track proxy-target substitution over time.
- Compare mission outcomes with metric-improvement campaigns.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Hicks et al. (2015) warns that quantitative information should not morph from an instrument into the goal and emphasizes combining statistics with sensitivity to research aims.

## Caveats

- This overlaps with systemic-effects concepts; use it specifically for role drift from evidence tool to optimization target.
- Some goals legitimately need numerical targets but still require construct validation.
