# Knowledge-use narrowing

## Summary

Knowledge-use narrowing is the mechanism that science and technology can become less disruptive when researchers draw on a narrower slice of available prior knowledge, even as the total stock of knowledge grows.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, patent, field, cohort, reference set, title/abstract vocabulary, or citation neighborhood.
- Typical representation: diversity of cited work, concentration in highly cited predecessors, self-citation or own-work reliance, reference-age pattern, or semantic/vocabulary diversity.
- Mechanism: the usable or attended knowledge base narrows relative to the expanding available knowledge base.
- Empirical signature: declining disruptiveness coincides with narrower cited knowledge, more familiar prior work, or stronger reliance on local predecessors.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Gives a mechanism-level account for [disruption and consolidation](disruption_consolidation.md) and long-run declines in disruptiveness.
- Connects [burden of knowledge](burden_of_knowledge.md), [attention inequality](attention_inequality.md), [reference-age search depth](../measures/reference_age_search_depth.md), and [reference-popularity search](../measures/reference_popularity_search.md).
- Evans (2008) adds an infrastructure-interface channel: [electronic access citation narrowing](electronic_access_citation_narrowing.md) can narrow knowledge use through search and hyperlink navigation even as availability expands.
- The Evans mechanism now splits into [online search-browse substitution](online_search_browse_substitution.md), [hyperlink consensus acceleration](hyperlink_consensus_acceleration.md), and [forced-browsing serendipity](forced_browsing_serendipity.md).
- Splits into operational measures for [cited work diversity](../measures/cited_work_diversity.md), [top-cited predecessor concentration](../measures/top_cited_predecessor_concentration.md), [cited predecessor semantic diversity](../measures/cited_predecessor_semantic_diversity.md), [title word diversity](../measures/title_word_diversity.md), and [title combinatorial novelty](../measures/title_combinatorial_novelty.md).
- Helps formulate policy experiments such as [broad-search time policy](broad_search_time_policy.md): broaden search tools, change review incentives, fund exploratory recombination, protect time to read widely, or expose older/less popular prior work.
- Provides a warning for large scholarly databases: more available knowledge does not guarantee broader actual use.
- Extends beyond citation reference lists to strategy choice when expanding opportunity sets are increasingly filtered toward repeat and local work.

## Operationalization

- Measure [cited work diversity](../measures/cited_work_diversity.md) or predecessor-patent diversity over time within fields.
- Track [top-cited predecessor concentration](../measures/top_cited_predecessor_concentration.md) and the share of references to own or closely related work.
- Track [online backfile depth](../measures/online_backfile_depth.md), [citation breadth via distinct cited sources](../measures/citation_breadth_distinct_sources.md), and [citation concentration Herfindahl](../measures/citation_concentration_herfindahl.md) when narrowing may be interface-driven.
- Compare [title word diversity](../measures/title_word_diversity.md), [title combinatorial novelty](../measures/title_combinatorial_novelty.md), and [cited predecessor semantic diversity](../measures/cited_predecessor_semantic_diversity.md).
- Model disruption scores as a function of knowledge-stock growth and knowledge-use breadth.
- Test whether interventions that broaden search also increase disruptive or frontier-expanding outputs.
- Validate trend interpretations against [citation network rewiring nulls](../methods/citation_network_rewiring_nulls.md) before attributing narrowing solely to behavior.
- Pair mechanism claims with [disruptiveness practice-control stack](../validations/disruptiveness_practice_control_stack.md) and [cross-corpus disruptiveness replication](../validations/cross_corpus_disruptiveness_replication.md) when source coverage or changing citation practices could drive the trend.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Evans (2008) reports that as more journal issues came online, cited articles became more recent, fewer journals and articles were cited, and more citations went to fewer journals and articles.
- Evans links this narrowing to a shift from print browsing and perusal toward online search and hyperlink following, which can accelerate consensus around prevailing cited work.
- Evans also frames poor print indexing as a source of incidental exposure that may have broadened comparisons and historical anchoring before online search made targeted retrieval easier.
- Verified full-text evidence from Park, Leahey, and Funk (2023) reports that papers and patents have become less disruptive over decades across large-scale paper and patent datasets.
- Park et al. link declining disruptiveness to narrower use of previous knowledge, including declining diversity of cited work and increased reliance on familiar knowledge.
- The paper distinguishes the available stock of knowledge from the effective stock being used: accumulated knowledge may enable discovery, but narrowing use can limit disruptive recombination.
- Park et al. report that declines remain after robustness checks for field-specific factors, publication/citation growth, citation practices, and alternative CD-index derivations.
- Their full text also separates the average decline from the stable absolute number of highly disruptive papers and patents, motivating [high-disruption tail conservation](high_disruption_tail_conservation.md) as a distinct pattern.
- Park et al. operationalize narrowing through declining diversity of cited work, rising concentration in the 1 percent most cited predecessors, falling semantic diversity among those predecessors, increasing self-citation, older cited work, and less diverse title language.
- Verified full-text evidence from Foster, Rzhetsky, and Evans (2015) adds a research-strategy version: biomedical chemistry's possible new and bridging links expanded rapidly, yet observed strategies increasingly favored repeat relationships and new consolidations over broader exploration.
- Foster et al.'s opportunity-normalized model estimates growing preference for established knowledge and local new consolidations, making search narrowing visible even before citation-reference patterns are analyzed.

## Caveats

- Narrower knowledge use can reflect specialization, method standardization, data availability, evaluation incentives, or database artifacts.
- Disruption decline does not mean there are no highly disruptive works; Park et al. report relative stability in the absolute number of highly disruptive papers and patents.
- Broadening cited knowledge can increase noise if researchers add distant references without substantive integration.
