# Postdoctoral mobility rite of passage

## Summary

Postdoctoral mobility rite of passage is the mechanism in which early-career institutional moves function as a normal broadening phase rather than as the same process that governs later-career mobility.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: scientist, career stage, institution move, postdoctoral phase, or affiliation trajectory.
- Typical representation: career-age mobility curve with a high early-career move probability.
- Mechanism: early scientists move to acquire training, networks, and placement opportunities before later constraints reduce mobility.
- Empirical signature: institutional moves are concentrated early in scientific careers and decline after the postdoctoral or early faculty period.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Interprets [career move timing distribution](../measures/career_move_timing_distribution.md).
- Adds a lifecycle mechanism to [scientific mobility](scientific_mobility.md).
- Helps separate career-stage effects from [career mobility distance decay](../measures/career_mobility_distance_decay.md).
- Provides a caution against treating all moves in an affiliation trajectory as behaviorally equivalent.

## Operationalization

- Reconstruct career age from first publication, first affiliation, degree date, or first observed institution.
- Identify institutional moves from affiliation changes.
- Estimate move probability by years since career start and compare early, mid, and late career windows.
- Interpret the early peak in light of postdoctoral training, job-market entry, and placement norms.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Deville, Wang, Sinatra, Song, Blondel, and Barabasi (2014) shows that mobility is most frequent early in scientists' careers.
- The paper interprets early moves partly as a postdoctoral period in which relocation and institutional change are common.
- Later-career mobility is described as less common, consistent with increasing professional and personal constraints.

## Caveats

- The postdoctoral phase differs across fields, countries, and historical periods.
- Publication-based career age can misclassify late entrants or scientists with missing early records.
- Early mobility can reflect selection into the observed cohort, not only a universal rite of passage.
