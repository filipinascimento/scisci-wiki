# PCS commercialization outcome expansion

## Summary

PCS commercialization outcome expansion uses public patent-paper links to connect scientific papers with startup, productivity, entrepreneurship, and employment outcomes beyond university patent and licensing datasets.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, patent-paper link, firm, startup, inventor, university, or outcome record.
- Typical representation: paper-to-patent-to-outcome linkage pipeline.
- Mechanism target: broaden observable commercialization pathways from formal university IP to publication-mediated technology use.
- Empirical signature: papers can be linked to downstream economic or organizational outcomes even when they are not themselves university patents.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [university-firm patent-paper handoff](university_firm_patent_paper_handoff.md).
- Connects [scientist-inventor boundary spanning](scientist_inventor_boundary_spanning.md) with non-university patent outcomes.
- Turns [patent-paper links](../datasets/patent_paper_links.md) into a bridge for economic datasets.
- Complements [commercial engagement consolidation](commercial_engagement_consolidation.md) by exposing broader commercialization traces.

## Operationalization

- Start from a confidence-thresholded patent-paper citation table.
- Link patents to assignees, inventors, technology classes, and application dates.
- Merge with startup, employment, productivity, licensing, or firm-performance records.
- Compare outcomes for cited papers against the [PCS companion publication denominator](../datasets/pcs_companion_publication_denominator.md).
- Distinguish paper-mediated diffusion from university-owned IP pathways.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Marx and Fuegi (2020) explicitly proposes using public PCS links to study entrepreneurship, productivity, employment, and other commercialization outcomes.
- The paper argues that broad patent-paper linkage can move beyond the narrow set of academic discoveries represented by university patents.
- This motif captures the downstream research program enabled by open PCS data.
- It also depends on confidence thresholds and outcome-linkage validation.

## Caveats

- Patent-paper links alone do not prove that the cited paper caused a firm outcome.
- Outcome datasets introduce their own coverage, linkage, and privacy limits.
- Commercialization outcomes can occur without patents and therefore remain only partially observed.
