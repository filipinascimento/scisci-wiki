# Disciplinary-perspective rigor-creativity

## Summary

Different disciplinary perspectives can contribute both rigor and creativity, making diversity valuable through structured difference rather than mere category count.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: discipline, perspective, research team, method, epistemic standard, or creative combination.
- Typical representation: perspective-diversity mechanism linking disciplinary distance to rigor and creative recombination.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: epistemic value of disciplinary diversity.
- Empirical signature: teams or portfolios with well-integrated disciplinary perspectives show broader problem framing or novel combinations without losing quality control.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Connects interdisciplinarity mechanism to [interdisciplinarity](interdisciplinarity.md) and [interdisciplinarity dimensions](../measures/interdisciplinarity_dimensions.md).
- Provides a reusable motif for comparing [scientific ecosystem diversity](scientific_ecosystem_diversity.md) with adjacent science-of-science constructs.
- Should be cross-referenced when interpreting [knowledge integration proxy boundary](../validations/knowledge_integration_proxy_boundary.md) in linked scholarly data.

## Operationalization

- Represent disciplines as perspectives with methods, standards, assumptions, and problem framings.
- Measure both disparity among perspectives and integration or quality-control mechanisms.
- Avoid equating many categories with useful interdisciplinary creativity unless perspective differences are substantively meaningful.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Stirling (2007) emphasizes variety, balance, and disparity as separate diversity properties and connects diversity to appraisal across knowledge systems.
- For science-of-science applications, disciplinary perspectives are one key source of disparity and creative recombination.

## Caveats

- Disciplinary distance can create coordination costs as well as creativity.
- Rigor and creativity are not guaranteed by diversity scores alone.
