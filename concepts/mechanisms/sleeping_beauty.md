# Sleeping beauty

## Summary

A sleeping beauty is a paper or idea that receives little attention for a long period and later experiences a sharp rise in recognition.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, patent, method, or idea.
- Typical representation: citation time series with a sleep period and awakening period.
- Mechanism: delayed field readiness, new application context, rediscovery, instrumentation, or boundary crossing.
- Empirical signature: long low-citation interval followed by sustained or abrupt citation increase.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Used to study delayed recognition and mismatch between immediate impact metrics and long-term knowledge value.
- Connects citation aging, field emergence, novelty, and interdisciplinary recombination.
- Provides cases for historical and mechanism-rich full-text reading.
- Can be split into [continuous delayed-recognition spectrum](continuous_delayed_recognition_spectrum.md), [cross-disciplinary awakening](cross_disciplinary_awakening.md), [awakening trigger citation](awakening_trigger_citation.md), [premature topic attention failure](premature_topic_attention_failure.md), [author-mediated awakening bridge](author_mediated_awakening_bridge.md), [benchmark-mediated rediscovery](benchmark_mediated_rediscovery.md), and [sleeping-beauty short-window bias](../validations/sleeping_beauty_short_window_bias.md).

## Operationalization

- Compute citation trajectories and identify sleep depth, sleep length, and awakening intensity.
- Compute the [beauty coefficient](../measures/beauty_coefficient.md) when a continuous delayed-recognition score is preferable to fixed thresholds.
- Validate awakenings with citation contexts and downstream field/topic changes.
- Separate delayed recognition from database coverage artifacts.

## Evidence and Validations

- van Raan introduced the motif in scientometrics; Ke et al. proposed broader quantitative identification.
- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) defines a sleeping beauty as a publication that goes unnoticed for a long period and then receives sudden attention, and introduces awakening as an empirical citation-history feature rather than only a metaphor.
- van Raan decomposes the motif into sleep depth, sleep length, and awake intensity, making the label operational rather than purely narrative.
- The same paper uses the [CWTS-ISI sleeping-beauty corpus](../datasets/cwts_isi_sleeping_beauty_corpus.md), derives an [awakening probability function](../measures/awakening_probability_function.md), identifies a trigger citation in the Romans/Polchinski case, and distinguishes sleeping beauties from already-cited classics.
- Its Romans/Polchinski case also motivates [affiliation-memory awakening bridge](affiliation_memory_awakening_bridge.md) as a case-level mechanism for how a dormant paper can be remembered when a field becomes ready.
- Verified full-text evidence from Wang, Song, and Barabasi (2013) shows how a shared citation-aging model can still produce delayed-impact trajectories when papers differ in immediacy and longevity, making sleeping beauties part of a broader citation-dynamics family.
- The associated [citation immediacy parameter](../measures/citation_immediacy_parameter.md) and [citation longevity parameter](../measures/citation_longevity_parameter.md) provide model-based neighbors to sleeping-beauty measures such as awakening time and beauty coefficient.
- Verified full-text evidence from Ke et al. (2015) supports sleeping beauties as a measurable trajectory type rather than only a historical anecdote: the paper introduces the beauty coefficient and awakening time for continuous delayed-recognition analysis.
- Ke et al. show that high delayed-recognition papers form a continuous spectrum, are not fully reproduced by simple citation null models, and often awaken through cross-disciplinary citation.
- Ke et al. also show three lower-level awakening mechanisms and contrast classes: top APS sleeping beauties can cluster around [premature topics](premature_topic_attention_failure.md), Garfield's citation-index paper shows an [author-mediated awakening bridge](author_mediated_awakening_bridge.md), Zachary's karate-club paper shows [benchmark-mediated rediscovery](benchmark_mediated_rediscovery.md), and negative B values identify early-peaking contrast trajectories.
- The Ke full text also supports the lower-level motifs [citation-model first-mover limit](../validations/citation_model_first_mover_limit.md), [sleeping-beauty cross-dataset visibility](../validations/sleeping_beauty_cross_dataset_visibility.md), and [awakening co-citation keyword diagnosis](../methods/awakening_cocitation_keyword_diagnosis.md).
- Verified full-text evidence from Clauset et al. (2017) treats sleeping beauties as a limit case for simple citation prediction: some papers become influential only after fields, tools, or downstream problems make their implications visible.
- Verified full-text evidence from Wu et al. (2019) uses delayed-attention measures to show that disruptive and small-team work can have slower uptake, linking sleeping-beauty behavior to [team size disruption](team_size_disruption.md).

## Caveats

- Definitions vary substantially across thresholds and time windows.
- Older papers can be affected by incomplete citation indexing.
- Extreme cases are rare relative to the annual publication population, so prevalence claims should report denominators and threshold severity.
