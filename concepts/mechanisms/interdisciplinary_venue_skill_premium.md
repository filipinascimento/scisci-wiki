# Interdisciplinary venue skill premium

## Summary

Broad elite venues may reward transferable publication craft and audience framing more strongly than field-specific expertise alone.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: venue, field, author team, or paper.
- Typical representation: chaperone-effect magnitude by interdisciplinary versus field-specific venue type.
- Mechanism: cross-field audience framing and venue craft create a premium for transferable publishing know-how.
- Empirical signature: chaperone effects are stronger in broad, interdisciplinary, or high-impact generalist venues.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Interprets [field chaperone-effect gradient](../measures/field_chaperone_effect_gradient.md) as a skill-premium mechanism.
- Connects venue-specific mentorship to [prestige-venue author specialization](prestige_venue_author_specialization.md).
- Useful when comparing specialized field journals with multidisciplinary generalist outlets.

## Operationalization

- Classify venues by interdisciplinarity, audience breadth, prestige tier, and field specificity.
- Estimate normalized chaperone magnitudes within venue classes and field panels.
- Compare whether prior venue experience predicts access more strongly in generalist venues after controls.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Sekara et al. (2018) reports field gradients in chaperone effects and interprets broad elite venues as places where publication know-how may be especially valuable.
- This supports a mechanism focused on transferable venue skill rather than only same-field expertise.

## Caveats

- High-impact generalist venues also differ in selectivity, audience size, and reputation effects.
- Interdisciplinary venue skill is inferred from accepted-publication patterns, not directly observed editorial learning.
