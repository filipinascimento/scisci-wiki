# English-language excellence equation

## Summary

Evaluation regimes can equate excellence with English-language, Web-of-Science-visible publication, making language coverage a mechanism of prestige and exclusion.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: publication, language, journal, field, evaluation rule, or citation database.
- Typical representation: language-indexing prestige pathway from database coverage to evaluation criteria.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: language-mediated exclusion in metric evaluation.
- Empirical signature: non-English or regional-language outputs lose evaluation value because excellence is operationalized through English indexed venues..

## Uses in Science of Science

## Operationalization

- Identify criteria equating excellence with English-language or high-impact indexed journals.
- Compare English and non-English output coverage.
- Test ranking changes after adding regional-language sources.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Hicks et al. (2015) describes settings where excellence is treated as English-language publication and impact factors depend on a mostly English-language Web of Science base.

## Caveats

- English publication can be appropriate for some global research aims.
- The failure mode is treating English-indexed publication as a universal excellence proxy.
