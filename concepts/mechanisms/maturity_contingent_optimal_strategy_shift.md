# Maturity-contingent optimal strategy shift

## Summary

Efficient search changes as a knowledge network matures, moving from central high-degree combinations toward more distant and disconnected exploration.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: knowledge network, discovery fraction, strategy parameter, degree, distance, disconnected component, or maturity level.
- Typical representation: phase-dependent strategy rule estimated across target-network discovery fractions.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: dynamic transition in optimal research strategy as fields mature.
- Empirical signature: central combinations can be efficient early, while full-network discovery requires more distant and less imitative search..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines knowledge-network search mechanism by linking it to [mature field exploration gap](mature_field_exploration_gap.md) and [preferential attachment](preferential_attachment.md).
- Useful as a reusable distinction when [topological cognitive distance proxy](../measures/topological_cognitive_distance_proxy.md) is analyzed in science-of-science data.

## Operationalization

- Estimate optimal strategies for increasing target-network discovery fractions.
- Track parameter trajectories for degree preference, distance, repetition, and disconnected-component bridging.
- Compare observed field maturity with the strategy that would maximize the chosen discovery objective.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Rzhetsky et al. (2015) finds high-degree combinations efficient early, while full-network discovery requires more distant links and less imitation of prior choices.
- The motif captures a dynamic transition rule rather than a static mature-field gap.

## Caveats

- Close to mature-field exploration-gap motifs; this page should be used for the phase transition.
- Optimal shifts depend on the chosen objective and the hidden-network assumptions.
