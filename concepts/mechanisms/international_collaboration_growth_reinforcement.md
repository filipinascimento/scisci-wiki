# International collaboration growth reinforcement

## Summary

Rising international coauthorship volume can reinforce emergent global collaboration structures and core-periphery patterns.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: country pair, international coauthorship share, network core, component, regional hub, or time period.
- Typical representation: time-comparative country collaboration network with density and core measures.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: structural reinforcement through growth of international collaboration.
- Empirical signature: international shares, core size, and density grow together while existing global structures become more pronounced.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines international-collaboration mechanism by linking it to [international coauthorship share](../measures/international_coauthorship_share.md) and [country collaboration core](../measures/country_collaboration_core.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [regional collaboration hubs](../measures/regional_collaboration_hubs.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [polycentric collaboration core competition](polycentric_collaboration_core_competition.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- Compare network density, core size, component structure, and regional hub emergence across time points.
- Test whether new international links amplify existing core/periphery patterns.
- Separate growth reinforcement from source-coverage and country-coding changes.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified local full text from Wagner and Leydesdorff (2005) reports international shares nearly doubling, cooperating-country cores expanding, and growth reinforcing global structures.
- The motif treats rising collaboration volume as a structural mechanism rather than only a descriptive trend.

## Caveats

- Two time points do not establish causality.
- Growth can reflect database coverage, capacity expansion, or policy changes as well as reinforcement.
