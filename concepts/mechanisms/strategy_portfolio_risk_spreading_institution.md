# Strategy-portfolio risk-spreading institution

## Summary

Institutions can improve discovery by evaluating and funding portfolios of experiments rather than isolated safe projects.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: funding institution, research group, project portfolio, risk mix, experiment strategy, or evaluation unit.
- Typical representation: portfolio-level evaluation mechanism over diverse experiment strategies.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: institutional risk spreading for collective discovery.
- Empirical signature: portfolio evaluation can tolerate risky experiments because failures are balanced across groups or programs..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines discovery-policy mechanism by linking it to [funding incentives and exploration](funding_incentives_and_exploration.md) and [funding autonomy exploration](funding_autonomy_exploration.md).

## Operationalization

- Identify policies that evaluate groups, people, or portfolios instead of isolated projects.
- Track strategy diversity, risk distribution, and discovery outcomes across the portfolio.
- Compare portfolio-level evaluation with single-project review under equal resource constraints.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Rzhetsky et al. (2015) points to Bell Labs-style group evaluation, HHMI-style person funding, and Grand Challenges-style support for radical ideas.
- The motif connects discovery-search models to institution-level risk spreading.

## Caveats

- The paper proposes these as policy implications rather than tested interventions.
- Portfolio evaluation can hide individual accountability or concentrate resources in elite groups.
