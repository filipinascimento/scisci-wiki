# Multidisciplinary venue delayed recognition

## Summary

Multidisciplinary venue delayed recognition is the mechanism hypothesis that broad-scope journals can publish work that is too premature or cross-cutting for immediate field uptake but later becomes important to emerging communities.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, multidisciplinary journal, field category, high-B subset, citing community, or awakening case.
- Typical representation: high-B share from multidisciplinary categories, delayed-recognition case list, or venue-category comparison.
- Mechanism: broad venues expose speculative or cross-field contributions that may wait for later fields, tools, or debates before being recognized.
- Empirical signature: multidisciplinary science categories appear prominently among high delayed-recognition papers.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds a venue-level mechanism to [sleeping beauty](sleeping_beauty.md) and [cross-disciplinary awakening](cross_disciplinary_awakening.md).
- Connects [sleeping-beauty field-origin profile](../measures/sleeping_beauty_field_origin_profile.md) to [field emergence](field_emergence.md) and [premature topic attention failure](premature_topic_attention_failure.md).
- Helps distinguish venue scope from field-category artifacts captured by [multidisciplinary field mix caveat](../validations/multidisciplinary_field_mix_caveat.md).
- Motivates case-level citation-context checks for delayed-recognition papers published in generalist outlets.

## Operationalization

- Identify high-B papers and their journal categories.
- Compare the presence of multidisciplinary science categories in the high-B set against lower-B or all-paper baselines.
- Examine whether post-awakening citations come from newly active or different fields.
- Use full-text citation contexts and co-citation shifts to distinguish broad venue exposure from classification artifacts.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Ke et al. (2015) profiles top sleeping beauties by Journal Citation Reports categories.
- The paper reports that multidisciplinary sciences rank highly among categories producing top delayed-recognition papers.
- Ke et al. interpret this as consistent with broad journals publishing work that can seem premature or futuristic but later becomes field-defining.

## Caveats

- Multidisciplinary category labels can conflate genuinely broad venues with classification convenience.
- High visibility can also create immediate attention, so broad venue publication is not sufficient for delayed recognition.
- Case-level reading is needed before attributing a specific awakening to venue scope.
