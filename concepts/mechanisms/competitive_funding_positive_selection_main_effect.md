# Competitive-funding positive selection main effect

## Summary

Competitive funding can show a positive average association with novelty before status heterogeneity is accounted for.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: research project, competitive-fund ratio, novelty score, OLS coefficient, status group, or funding source.
- Typical representation: main-effect estimate for competitive-funding share predicting novelty.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: average selection or treatment association of competitive funding with novelty.
- Empirical signature: competitively funded projects can appear more novel overall even when low-status groups experience stronger constraints..

## Uses in Science of Science

## Operationalization

- Estimate main-effect models for competitive-fund indicators or ratios predicting novelty.
- Compare main effects with status-interaction models.
- Interpret positive coefficients against selection, review, and resource channels rather than as a simple universal treatment effect.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wang et al. (2018) reports a positive significant main effect for competitive funding before status interactions.
- The result contrasts with the authors' first hypothesis and motivates status-contingency interpretation.

## Caveats

- Close to funding selection-treatment splits; keep this as a result node only when main-effect interpretation matters.
- Positive selection into competitive funding can mimic a treatment effect.
