# Collective discovery incentive misalignment

## Summary

Collective discovery incentive misalignment occurs when individually rational research choices generate steady outputs but slow field-level discovery of the remaining knowledge network.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: scientist, field, funding system, evaluation regime, research strategy, or knowledge-network search process.
- Typical representation: divergence between career-friendly observed strategies and collectively efficient search strategies.
- Mechanism: individual publication, citation, tenure, and grant incentives favor central, coherent, lower-risk work even when mature networks require riskier distant search.
- Empirical signature: observed strategies become more conservative while simulations show that more diverse or riskier strategies would discover more new relationships.

## Operationalization

- Estimate observed strategy parameters and compare them with optimized collective-discovery strategies.
- Measure whether local career outputs, citations, awards, or funding success reward different strategies from those that minimize field-level discovery cost.
- Model interventions that shift evaluation from individual experiments to portfolios or groups.
- Track whether interventions increase strategy diversity, distant search, disconnected-component bridging, and failure reporting.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Rzhetsky et al. (2015) argues that MEDLINE-like strategies support coherent local exploration and steady scientific careers but become inefficient for science as a whole.
- The paper states that individual productivity incentives, reinforced by institutions such as tenure, may conflict with science's collective interest in maximizing discovery.
- Rzhetsky et al. recommend policy shifts that subsidize risky strategies, incentivize strategy diversity, and encourage publication of failed experiments.
- Suggested institutional examples include group-level evaluation, funding promising individuals rather than projects, and programs that lower barriers to radical ideas.

## Caveats

- Collective efficiency is defined relative to a chosen objective function, and novel edge discovery is not the only scientific value.
- Riskier strategies can increase failures, costs, ethical concerns, and false starts.
- If risky failures are unpublished, [unpublished-failure censoring](../validations/unpublished_failure_censoring.md) can make observed strategy distributions look safer than actual attempted work.
- Incentive reforms must preserve replication, robustness, translation, and safety alongside exploration.
- [Innovation-verification resource balance](innovation_verification_resource_balance.md) is the replication-specific version of this caveat.
