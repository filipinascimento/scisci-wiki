# Doctoral-elite agenda and demography transmission

## Summary

Concentrated doctoral training pipelines can transmit research agendas and demographic composition into the broader scientific workforce.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: doctoral program, faculty hire, research agenda, demographic composition, placement network, or field workforce.
- Typical representation: training-origin transmission pathway through faculty hiring networks.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: agenda and demographic imprint of elite doctoral pipelines.
- Empirical signature: a small set of doctoral programs disproportionately shapes field topics and workforce composition through placement..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Links [faculty doctoral origin composition](../measures/faculty_doctoral_origin_composition.md) to field-level agenda outcomes.
- Extends hierarchy work beyond placement counts.
- Provides a mechanism for demographic and topic stratification.

## Operationalization

- Measure faculty production concentration by doctoral institution and downstream placement hierarchy.
- Link origin-program topic portfolios and demographic composition to field-level workforce outcomes.
- Test attenuation or amplification across hiring cohorts.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Clauset et al. (2017) says a small set of prestigious doctoral programs trains most career researchers and shapes research agendas, doctoral-student demographics, and workforce composition.

## Caveats

- Transmission is plausible from the described structure but causal pathways require follow-up analysis.
- Program influence can operate through selection, training, networks, or resources.
