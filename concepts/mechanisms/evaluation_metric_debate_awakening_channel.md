# Evaluation-metric debate awakening channel

## Summary

A dormant paper can awaken when later debates about research evaluation make it newly central.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: sleeping paper, evaluation metric, journal impact factor, citing debate, author-mediated citation, or awakening period.
- Typical representation: case-based channel linking metric debates to delayed citation growth.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: policy and metric-debate context as an awakening mechanism.
- Empirical signature: post-awakening citations concentrate around research-evaluation and metric critique contexts..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines awakening channel by linking it to [author mediated awakening bridge](author_mediated_awakening_bridge.md) and [journal impact factor](../measures/journal_impact_factor.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [journal impact factor assessment misuse](../validations/journal_impact_factor_assessment_misuse.md) is interpreted from citation histories.
- Adds cross-links to [awakening trigger citation](awakening_trigger_citation.md) so delayed-recognition and ranking motifs stay connected.

## Operationalization

- For high-B cases, test whether post-awakening citations concentrate around metric critique, journal-impact-factor terms, or evaluation-policy works.
- Separate author-mediated rediscovery from broader debate-driven uptake.
- Compare citation contexts before and after the metric-debate inflection.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Ke et al. (2015) describes Garfield's 1955 citation-index paper awakening around 2000 through later Garfield papers and influential work on HITS and journal impact-factor limitations.
- The motif records a policy and metric-debate channel for delayed recognition.

## Caveats

- The evidence is case-specific and overlaps author-mediated awakening.
- Metric debate may amplify an already awakened paper rather than trigger the awakening.
