# Team visibility-credit tradeoff

## Summary

Team visibility-credit tradeoff is the mechanism by which teamwork can raise a paper's impact through coauthor-network visibility while making individual credit allocation more difficult.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: team-authored paper, coauthor network, credit allocation, citation outcome, or researcher career.
- Typical representation: impact gain versus credit dilution or credit ambiguity.
- Mechanism: more coauthors can diffuse work through more networks, but shared authorship obscures individual contribution.
- Empirical signature: team-authored work receives more attention while credit must be allocated across multiple contributors.

## Operationalization

- Measure team size, coauthor network reach, and citation outcomes.
- Estimate whether coauthor visibility explains part of the team citation premium.
- Compare paper-level impact with author-level credit allocation.
- Distinguish collaboration benefits from authorship inflation or honorary authorship.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Fortunato et al. (2018) reports that team-authored papers are more likely to receive very high citation counts.
- The review suggests that more collaborations can increase visibility because coauthors introduce the work to their networks.
- It also emphasizes that credit within a team must be shared and that individual contributions are difficult to distinguish.

## Caveats

- Visibility benefits can vary with field, author order, prestige, and platform.
- Credit ambiguity is not the same as equal contribution.
- Team citation premiums can also reflect resources, topic choice, and selection into large teams.
