# Awakening-threshold floor sensitivity

## Summary

Sleeping-beauty counts depend strongly on the minimum wake-intensity threshold.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: wake-intensity threshold, citation bin, sleeping-beauty count, sleep length, sleep depth, or threshold grid.
- Typical representation: threshold-sensitivity check over lower wake-intensity floors.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: robustness of delayed-recognition prevalence to wake thresholds.
- Empirical signature: lowering the wake threshold yields many more less-prominent sleeping beauties..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines sleeping-beauty threshold validation by linking it to [sleeping beauty threshold grid](../methods/sleeping_beauty_threshold_grid.md) and [sleeping beauty population rarity](../measures/sleeping_beauty_population_rarity.md).

## Operationalization

- Recompute counts while varying the lower wake-intensity floor below or above the first class of 21 citations over four years.
- Report sensitivity jointly with sleep length and sleep-depth thresholds.
- Distinguish prominent sleeping beauties from a broader continuous delayed-recognition spectrum.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) starts wake classes at 21 citations and notes that lowering sleep time, sleep depth, or awake intensity yields many more less-prominent sleeping beauties.
- The motif documents threshold dependence in early sleeping-beauty measurement.

## Caveats

- The paper demonstrates threshold dependence descriptively but does not run a full formal grid beyond reported classes.
- Thresholds can convert a continuous phenomenon into sparse categories.
