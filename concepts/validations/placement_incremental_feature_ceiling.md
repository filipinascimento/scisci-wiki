# Faculty-placement incremental-feature ceiling

## Summary

Rich early-career feature sets may add only limited predictive value beyond academic-pedigree baselines for faculty placement.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: faculty placement model, academic pedigree, early-career feature, covariate, or placement outcome.
- Typical representation: incremental-lift benchmark against pedigree-only placement models.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: added predictive value beyond doctoral or prestige baseline.
- Empirical signature: additional features slightly improve placement prediction over academic pedigree alone..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [faculty placement pedigree baseline](faculty_placement_pedigree_baseline.md).
- Acts as a case of [latent factor prediction ceiling](latent_factor_prediction_ceiling.md).
- Clarifies what faculty-placement models actually learn.

## Operationalization

- Benchmark placement models against pedigree-only and prestige-rank baselines.
- Report incremental lift from productivity, postdoctoral training, geography, gender, and other covariates.
- Stratify by discipline and placement definition.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Clauset et al. (2017) states that models using early productivity, postdoctoral training, geography, gender, and related data only slightly outperform knowing academic pedigree.

## Caveats

- Exact lift depends on dataset, discipline, and placement definition.
- Predicting placement is not the same as measuring merit.
