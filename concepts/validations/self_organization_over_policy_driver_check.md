# Self-organization over policy-driver check

## Summary

International collaboration explanations should test whether bottom-up self-organization explains network structure better than top-down policy or field-cost drivers.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: subfield, collaboration regime, policy driver, preferential attachment, network topology, or explanatory quadrant.
- Typical representation: organizing-driver comparison grid with self-organization indicators.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: validation of self-organization mechanisms in global collaboration.
- Empirical signature: preferential-attachment signatures appear across fields even where organizing drivers differ.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines international-collaboration validation by linking it to [international collaboration explanation matrix](../methods/international_collaboration_explanation_matrix.md) and [collaboration organizing driver quadrants](../representations/collaboration_organizing_driver_quadrants.md).
- Useful as a reusable check when [international collaboration preferential attachment](../mechanisms/international_collaboration_preferential_attachment.md) is used in science-of-science inference.
- Creates cross-links to [constructed collaboration sustainability limit](constructed_collaboration_sustainability_limit.md) so the motif is not interpreted in isolation.

## Operationalization

- Compare network signatures across organizing-driver quadrants.
- Test whether preferential attachment appears in both top-down and bottom-up fields.
- Audit alternative explanations such as specialization, capacity, and policy construction.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified local full text from Wagner and Leydesdorff (2005) expected organizing drivers to matter but concludes preferential attachment across fields had overtaken those drivers during the 1990s.
- The motif converts that conclusion into a validation check for self-organization claims.

## Caveats

- The paper does not fully disprove specialization or capacity explanations.
- Preferential-attachment signatures can be consistent with several mechanisms.
