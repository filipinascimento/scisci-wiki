# Altmetric residual-category caveat

## Summary

Altmetric residual-category caveat is the warning that altmetrics group heterogeneous traces mainly because they are not traditional bibliometric citations, not because they share one mechanism or meaning.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: altmetric source, attention event, platform trace, policy mention, news mention, or social-media event.
- Typical representation: heterogeneous source families grouped under a noncitation label.
- Validation target: prevent altmetric evidence from being interpreted as a single construct.
- Empirical signature: analyses separate source families or explicitly state what each source family is expected to measure.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Qualifies [altmetrics](../datasets/altmetrics.md).
- Connects [social media attention channels](../datasets/social_media_attention_channels.md) with broader noncitation traces.
- Helps interpret [altmetric source factor structure](../representations/altmetric_source_factor_structure.md).
- Supports source-specific rather than all-altmetric impact claims.

## Operationalization

- List the source families included in an altmetric dataset.
- State the construct each family is supposed to represent.
- Analyze policy, news, social media, reference managers, and other traces separately when possible.
- Avoid treating a composite altmetric score as mechanism-homogeneous.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Haunschild and Bornmann (2017) defines altmetrics as nontraditional indicators and stresses their heterogeneity.
- The paper treats policy-document mentions as one specific altmetric channel rather than as equivalent to all other online attention.
- This supports interpreting altmetrics as a residual umbrella requiring source-level decomposition.

## Caveats

- Some projects deliberately use composite indicators for monitoring rather than construct purity.
- Source-family labels can change as providers add, remove, or redefine tracked platforms.
- Heterogeneity does not mean altmetrics are uninformative; it means their interpretation must be source-specific.
