# Observable setback-mechanism battery

## Summary

Observable setback-mechanism battery audits whether measured changes in topics, collaborations, research direction, author role, or institutional mobility explain the apparent effect of an early-career setback.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: researcher, paper, topic, collaborator, author role, institution, mechanism proxy, or performance gap.
- Typical representation: battery of observable mechanism tests after a near-threshold setback.
- Validation target: determine whether alternative observable channels explain survivor-conditioned performance differences.
- Empirical signature: measured channels either do not differ enough or do not account for the outcome gap.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [setback alternative-mechanism audit](setback_alternative_mechanism_audit.md).
- Complements [setback screening-effect check](setback_screening_effect_check.md) by testing mechanisms among survivors rather than selection alone.
- Links early-career setbacks to topic choice, collaboration, institutional mobility, and leadership-role traces.

## Operationalization

- Define observable candidate mechanisms before interpreting residual performance gaps.
- Measure topic shifts, hot-topic orientation, collaboration patterns, lead-author roles, research-direction changes, and institutional moves.
- Compare near-miss and narrow-win survivors on each channel.
- Reassess the main outcome gap after accounting for the measured channels.
- Record channels that remain suggestive but insufficient.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wang, Jones, and Wang (2019) examines whether the near-miss advantage is explained by changes in hot topics, collaborations, research directions, or institutional locus.
- The paper reports some suggestive evidence around hot-topic tendency but concludes that the observable battery does not explain the performance gap.
- Wang et al. use this audit alongside screening checks, matching, and RD estimates to narrow alternative explanations.

## Caveats

- Observable batteries cannot capture unmeasured motivation, mentoring, grit, local support, or private strategy changes.
- Mechanism proxies can be noisy and correlated.
- A battery that fails to explain the gap does not prove a single positive resilience mechanism.
