# Null-original replication adjudication

## Summary

Replication-success labels become ambiguous when the original focal result is null, because success criteria designed for positive findings no longer map cleanly to same-direction significance.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: original result, null effect, replication result, adjudication rule, or success label.
- Typical representation: special-case adjudication rule for original null findings.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: truth-status boundary for replication indicators.
- Empirical signature: standard success indicators provide conflicting or uninformative labels for originally null effects.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Connects replication outcome validation to [subjective replication judgment](../measures/subjective_replication_judgment.md) and [replication success indicator suite](../measures/replication_success_indicator_suite.md).
- Provides a reusable motif for comparing [same direction significance vote counting](../measures/same_direction_significance_vote_counting.md) with adjacent science-of-science constructs.
- Should be cross-referenced when interpreting [replication reproducibility](replication_reproducibility.md) in linked scholarly data.

## Operationalization

- Flag focal effects whose original result is null or not directionally significant.
- Specify whether replication success means confirming no detectable effect, falling inside equivalence bounds, or matching the original estimate.
- Keep null-original cases separate in headline replication rates when criteria are not comparable.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Open Science Collaboration (2015) includes subjective judgments and multiple success indicators rather than one universal rule.
- The design makes null or weak original results an adjudication boundary for binary replication success.

## Caveats

- Equivalence testing requires design choices not always available in original studies.
- A null replication of a null original can still be underpowered.
