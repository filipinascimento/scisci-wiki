# Expert-comment prematurity validation

## Summary

Expert-comment prematurity validation uses comments from original actors or knowledgeable domain experts to corroborate that a delayed-recognition case was genuinely ahead of its field.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: sleeping-beauty case, author comment, citing author comment, expert recollection, or historical interpretation.
- Typical representation: qualitative case validation attached to a quantitative delayed-recognition candidate.
- Validation target: distinguish genuine prematurity from citation artifacts, rediscovery accidents, or ordinary delayed diffusion.
- Empirical signature: knowledgeable participants report that the field was not ready for the dormant work when it appeared.

## Operationalization

- Identify extreme delayed-recognition candidates from citation trajectories.
- Collect comments from original authors, awakening-citation authors, or field experts.
- Compare comments with the timing and content of the awakening citation.
- Record whether the comments support prematurity, rediscovery, institutional memory, or another mechanism.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) uses comments from Romans and Polchinski to interpret the most extreme sleeping-beauty case.
- The paper states that those comments made clear the case reflected being ahead of one's time.
- The acknowledgment also documents direct consultation with the scientists involved in the case.

## Caveats

- Expert recollections are retrospective and can be shaped by later fame.
- Comments validate case interpretation, not population-level prevalence.
- Multiple expert sources are preferable when possible.
