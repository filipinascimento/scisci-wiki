# Resource-intensive replication undercoverage

## Summary

Replication portfolios can underrepresent studies that require specialized populations, costly interventions, rare equipment, or long execution timelines.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: target study, feasibility constraint, replication cost, excluded article, or sampling-frame stratum.
- Typical representation: feasibility attrition audit for high-resource target studies.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: coverage bias in empirical reproducibility estimates.
- Empirical signature: resource-intensive original studies have higher attrition or lower completion rates than simpler studies.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Connects replication coverage validation to [replication feasibility attrition](replication_feasibility_attrition.md) and [replication denominator scope limit](replication_denominator_scope_limit.md).
- Provides a reusable motif for comparing [psychology reproducibility sampling frame](../datasets/psychology_reproducibility_sampling_frame.md) with adjacent science-of-science constructs.
- Should be cross-referenced when interpreting [replication execution challenge gradient](replication_execution_challenge_gradient.md) in linked scholarly data.

## Operationalization

- Code feasibility barriers such as equipment, population access, intervention cost, duration, and specialized expertise.
- Compare completed and noncompleted targets by resource intensity.
- Report reproducibility estimates with denominator notes for hard-to-replicate study classes.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Open Science Collaboration (2015) documents feasibility attrition in the replication project and limits the final denominator to completed direct replications.
- The project design implies that resource-intensive studies can be less represented in final estimates.

## Caveats

- Undercoverage does not imply that excluded studies are less reproducible.
- Feasibility constraints are field-specific and can change with funding or infrastructure.
