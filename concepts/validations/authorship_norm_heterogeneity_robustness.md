# Authorship-norm heterogeneity robustness

## Summary

Team-growth and team-impact patterns should be checked across fields with different authorship ordering, postdoctoral labor, and grant-based research norms.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: field, authorship norm, grant dependence, postdoctoral labor prevalence, team trend, or RTI estimate.
- Typical representation: field-stratified robustness grid over authorship-norm environments.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: robustness of team-production trends to heterogeneous byline conventions.
- Empirical signature: team growth and impact patterns persist across fields with different byline conventions and research labor systems.

- Refines team-growth robustness validation by linking it to [authorship threshold drift caveat](authorship_threshold_drift_caveat.md) and [team growth driver portfolio](../mechanisms/team_growth_driver_portfolio.md).

## Operationalization

- Stratify fields by authorship-order conventions, postdoc prevalence, and grant-based research intensity where metadata allow.
- Compare team shares, mean size, and impact ratios across strata.
- Pair the check with authorship-threshold drift and contribution-statement evidence when available.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified local full text from Wuchty et al. (2007) argues that results hold across fields where order of authorship, postdoctoral prevalence, and grant-based research differ substantially.
- This supports a robustness motif rather than a single-field authorship convention explanation.

## Caveats

- The check is not a direct causal test of authorship norms.
- Contribution statements or policy histories would be needed for stronger validation.
