# Intermediate disruptiveness entry

## Summary

Intermediate disruptiveness entry is the validation pattern that post-shock entrants can renew a field through durable moderate shifts rather than through highly disruptive overturning.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: entrant paper, subfield, disruption index bin, citation-impact bin, event-study outcome, or field-renewal mechanism.
- Typical representation: treatment effects by disruption-index range, consolidation-disruption scale, or high-citation moderate-disruption bin.
- Validation target: separate intellectual rejuvenation from full paradigm displacement.
- Empirical signature: entrant effects concentrate in intermediate disruption ranges, not only in highly disruptive or purely consolidating papers.

- Prevents overclaiming that post-star entry produces wholesale Kuhnian revolutions.

## Operationalization

- Compute a disruption or consolidation-disruption score for post-event entrant papers.
- Bin entrant papers into consolidating, intermediate, and highly disruptive ranges.
- Estimate treatment effects separately by disruption bin and citation-impact bin.
- Compare disruption-bin patterns with reference-vintage and MeSH-vintage renewal measures.
- Interpret intermediate-disruption effects as directional field renewal unless there is separate evidence of paradigm replacement.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Azoulay, Fons-Rosen, and Graff Zivin (2019) examines whether post-death non-collaborator contributions are disruptive.
- The paper reports stronger entrant patterns in intermediate disruption ranges than in the most disruptive tail.
- Its discussion characterizes the renewal as many small, permanent revolutions rather than wholesale overthrow of the subfield.
- This distinguishes entrant rejuvenation from a simple disruption-maximization story.

## Caveats

- Disruption scores depend on citation-window choices and reference-set construction.
- Intermediate disruption does not prove the entrant work is epistemically better.
- A field can be renewed through methods, data, or applications that disruption indices only partially capture.
- The pattern should be interpreted alongside citation-tail, reference-vintage, and topic-vintage evidence.
