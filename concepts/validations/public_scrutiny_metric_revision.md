# Public scrutiny metric revision

## Summary

Transparent indicator systems should let technical critique trigger documented revisions to formulas or calculations.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: indicator formula, public critique, version change, recalculation, or metric protocol.
- Typical representation: public issue and revision loop for metric calculation rules.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: contestable and correctable metric construction.
- Empirical signature: public critique of an indicator's technical properties produces a documented formula or calculation revision..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds a public correction mechanism to [transparent metric construction](../methods/transparent_metric_construction.md).
- Operationalizes [indicator lifecycle review](../methods/indicator_lifecycle_review.md).
- Links metric revision to [scholarly snapshot versioning](../methods/scholarly_snapshot_versioning.md).

## Operationalization

- Maintain public issue logs for metric critiques.
- Version indicator formulas and calculation code.
- Publish before/after sensitivity analyses when revisions occur.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Hicks et al. (2015) notes that published protocols enabled public scrutiny and that debate over an indicator's technical properties led to revised calculation.

## Caveats

- Revisions can reduce longitudinal comparability.
- Prospective application rules are needed when revised formulas affect past rankings.
