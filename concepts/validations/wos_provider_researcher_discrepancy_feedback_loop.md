# WoS provider-researcher discrepancy feedback loop

## Summary

Research partnerships with data providers can function as data-quality loops when researchers report discrepancies, methods, and findings back to the provider.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: data discrepancy, provider response, research partnership, correction note, or project report.
- Typical representation: provider-facing discrepancy log and feedback workflow.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: correction and clarification loop between WoS provider and external researchers.
- Empirical signature: suspected data issues and methodological misunderstandings are reported, reviewed, and sometimes corrected before or after publication..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds provider feedback to [research-ready table curation](../methods/research_ready_table_curation.md).
- Links [citation database coverage sensitivity](citation_database_coverage_sensitivity.md) to correction workflows.
- Useful for documenting conflicts of interest in provider-assisted analyses.

## Operationalization

- Track suspected data issues and provider responses as validation artifacts.
- Require end-of-project reports on methodology, data use, findings, and observations.
- Preserve pre-publication correction notes separately from final analytic claims.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Birkle et al. (2020) describes partner feedback, independent testing of WoS systems, reporting of methods and discrepancies, and provider correction of misunderstandings before publication.

## Caveats

- Provider feedback is useful but not independent adjudication.
- Provider involvement and possible conflicts should be documented.
