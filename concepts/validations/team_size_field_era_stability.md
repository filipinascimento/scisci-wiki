# Team-size field-era stability

## Summary

Checks whether the team-size disruption gradient survives stratification by historical period and discipline.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: field-period cell and team-size slope.
- Typical representation: meta-analysis or heat map of disruption-team-size slopes across fields and eras.
- Validation target: robustness of team-size disruption patterns across disciplines and periods.
- Empirical signature: most field-era cells show the same negative team-size/disruption gradient.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Adds heterogeneity checks to [team size and disruption](../mechanisms/team_size_disruption.md).
- Useful when broad cross-domain averages could hide field-specific exceptions.
- Connects to [team-size distribution contrast tests](team_size_distribution_contrast_tests.md).

## Operationalization

- Estimate disruption-team-size slopes within field-period cells.
- Meta-analyze heterogeneity and report exceptions, sparse cells, and confidence bands.
- Separate broad field stability from subfield, venue, and document-type exceptions.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wu et al. (2019) reports that the team-size disruption pattern is stable across eras and about 90% of disciplines.
- The statement supports a reusable field-era robustness check.

## Caveats

- Broad field stability can hide subfield, venue, and document-type exceptions.
- Sparse field-era cells may need smoothing but smoothing can hide exceptions.
