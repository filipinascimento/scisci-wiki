# Last-author PI role assumption

## Summary

Last-author PI role assumption is the operational shortcut that treats the final byline position as the principal investigator or senior mentor role in fields where author order encodes contribution and seniority.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: paper, author position, senior author, principal investigator, field, or author-order convention.
- Typical representation: last author as PI proxy, first author as junior contributor proxy, and nonlast author as prior junior exposure.
- Validation target: state and test whether author position can be used as a role proxy for venue, career, or mentorship analysis.
- Empirical signature: results depend on fields where the last-author convention is meaningful and survive sensitivity checks for alphabetical or non-seniority bylines.

## Operationalization

- Specify the field and period in which the last-author role convention is assumed.
- Treat last author as PI or senior author only after excluding or modeling alphabetical and consortium authorship where relevant.
- Treat prior nonlast same-journal appearance as junior or non-PI venue exposure.
- Run robustness checks using alphabetical-order nulls, nonalphabetical subsets, field stratification, or contribution statements when available.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Sekara et al. (2018) states that in biological and increasingly physical sciences the first author is often an early-career scientist and the last author is often a mentor figure.
- The paper uses author order to study experience in scientific multiauthor publications.
- Sekara et al. explicitly assume that the PI is listed last, while noting that the analysis is not affected if some papers do not mirror seniority roles because robustness checks are used.
- The study then divides last authors into new, chaperoned, and established PI categories.

## Caveats

- Last-author seniority is field-specific and can fail in alphabetical, economics-style, consortium, or equal-contribution authorship.
- Last authorship can mix supervision, funding, correspondence, conceptual leadership, or honorary authorship.
- Validation should be repeated when applying the proxy outside the fields and journals where it was originally calibrated.
