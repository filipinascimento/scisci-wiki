# Random-impact robustness grid

## Summary

A reusable validation battery for rerunning random-impact claims across career length, decade, solo/team samples, subareas, no-filter samples, impact definitions, and disciplines.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: career trajectory, robustness axis, shuffled null, or peak-position test.
- Typical representation: grid of observed-versus-shuffled tests across robustness axes.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: robustness of random placement of top-impact papers within careers.
- Empirical signature: top-impact sequence-position results persist across robustness axes..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Extends [random impact rule](../mechanisms/random_impact_rule.md) validation.
- Useful for reproducing [within-career impact shuffle null](within_career_impact_shuffle_null.md) claims.
- Links productivity-exposure and authorship-credit checks into one grid.

## Operationalization

- Produce observed-versus-shuffled peak-position tests for each robustness axis.
- Include career length, decade, solo/team samples, subareas, no-filter samples, impact definitions, and disciplines.
- Report failures and sparse axes rather than only pooled results.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Sinatra et al. (2016) reports robustness of random-impact claims across career length, decade, solo/team samples, subareas, no-filter samples, impact definitions, and disciplines.
- This supports a reusable robustness grid.

## Caveats

- Passing listed axes does not rule out unmeasured selection or database artifacts.
- Robustness grids can become underpowered in small subfields.
