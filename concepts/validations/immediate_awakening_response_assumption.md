# Immediate awakening-response assumption

## Summary

A threshold-based sleeping-beauty design assumes that awakening appears in the four years immediately after the sleep period.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: sleep period, wake window, citation count, threshold, candidate paper, or recognition lag.
- Typical representation: adjacent post-sleep window assumption for wake-intensity measurement.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: temporal validity of awakening classification.
- Empirical signature: papers are classified by citations in the immediately adjacent four-year window after sleep..

## Operationalization

- Classify awakening from citations accumulated in the adjacent four-year post-sleep window.
- Compare results with delayed, rolling, or wider wake-window definitions.
- Report whether gradual or multi-stage recognitions are excluded by the adjacent-window rule.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) measures wake intensity in a four-year period immediately following each sleeping period.
- The motif marks a design assumption behind threshold-based sleeping-beauty counts.

## Caveats

- The rule can miss gradual awakenings, additional-lag awakenings, and multi-stage recognition histories.
- It trades interpretability for temporal rigidity.
