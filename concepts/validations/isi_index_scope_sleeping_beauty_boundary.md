# ISI-index scope sleeping-beauty boundary

## Summary

Sleeping-beauty prevalence should be interpreted as a claim about indexed science when the scan is limited to ISI databases.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: indexed paper, ISI database, SCI, SSCI, AHCI, citation edge, or coverage boundary.
- Typical representation: corpus-boundary statement for ISI-indexed delayed-recognition searches.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: coverage validity of sleeping-beauty prevalence estimates.
- Empirical signature: the scan frame includes ISI-indexed literature rather than all scholarly outputs..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines sleeping-beauty corpus validation by linking it to [cwts isi sleeping beauty corpus](../datasets/cwts_isi_sleeping_beauty_corpus.md) and [web of science](../datasets/web_of_science.md).

## Operationalization

- Record whether the sleeping-beauty scan is limited to SCI, SSCI, AHCI, and related ISI indexes.
- Treat prevalence estimates as index-scope claims unless non-ISI coverage is audited.
- Compare field and document-type coverage against alternative databases when coverage bias matters.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) says the data system is based on SCI, SSCI, AHCI, and related ISI indexes.
- The motif makes index scope visible before interpreting rarity or field patterns.

## Caveats

- The paper presents the data system as broad science literature but does not test non-ISI coverage bias.
- Coverage boundaries can affect older, regional, book, and non-English sleeping beauties.
