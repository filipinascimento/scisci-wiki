# Sleeping-beauty sparse-tail cell fragility

## Summary

The highest wake-intensity and deepest-sleep threshold cells can contain zero or one publications, making tail estimates fragile.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: threshold cell, sleeping-beauty count, wake-intensity bin, sleep-depth bin, sleep length, or tail estimate.
- Typical representation: sparse-cell diagnostic for high-awakening sleeping-beauty count matrices.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: tail stability of rare delayed-recognition classifications.
- Empirical signature: high-awakening deep-sleep cells are dominated by zeros and singletons..

## Operationalization

- Flag threshold cells where the count is zero or one, especially high-cw, deep-sleep, and long-sleep combinations.
- Report tail-cell counts before estimating slopes or comparing fields.
- Pool cells or use uncertainty intervals when sparse cells drive claims.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from van Raan (2004) shows high-awakening cells in a 1988 long-sleep table that include zeros and singletons.
- The motif marks fragility in the tail of the sleeping-beauty count matrix.

## Caveats

- The table is an example slice and should be checked on the full measurement set.
- Sparse cells can be meaningful rare events or unstable artifacts of thresholds.
