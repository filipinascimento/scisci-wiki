# Option Scale Constraint Effects

## Summary

Option scale constraint effects are portfolio-diversity caveats in which option shares face resource limits, learning, scale economies, depletion, or other share-dependent performance changes.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: portfolio option, technology share, research program share, performance frontier, or portfolio constraint.
- Typical representation: constraint and nonlinear performance layer attached to diversity-performance appraisal.
- Validation target: prevent diversity-performance comparisons from assuming that option value is independent of scale.
- Empirical signature: the same option has different cost, impact, or feasibility as its portfolio contribution rises.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Qualifies [performance-space value representation](../representations/performance_space_value_representation.md).

- Helps science-policy portfolio analyses distinguish option diversity from feasible portfolio composition.

## Operationalization

- Record minimum, maximum, or feasible shares for each portfolio option.
- Model learning, scale, depletion, or resource-limit effects as option contribution changes.
- Separate pairwise interactions from option-specific scale effects.
- Show how constraints shift diversity-performance trade-offs.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Stirling (2007) states that portfolio appraisal can involve constraints on individual element contributions and portfolio effects from interactions.
- In the energy-technology illustration, Stirling notes learning or scale reductions, depletion-driven increases, and resource constraints as contributions rise.
- The paper frames diversity-performance trade-offs as requiring consideration of system constraints and interactions.

## Caveats

- Constraint estimates can be uncertain and politically contested.
- Scale effects can interact with pairwise portfolio synergies, so the two layers should not be blended without explanation.
- A constraint-aware portfolio may be less diverse for defensible feasibility reasons.
