# Open-access reward fee-support precondition

## Summary

Open-access reward fee-support precondition is the implementation check that OA-based researcher assessment should be paired with institutional fee support and before/after reach evaluation.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: open-access policy, promotion criterion, institutional OA fund, researcher output portfolio, or article-processing-charge support.
- Typical representation: OA reward criterion plus fee-support policy and reach/outcome monitoring.
- Validation target: distinguish rewarded openness from resource access to pay OA fees.
- Empirical signature: OA output increases are interpreted together with fund availability, citation or altmetric reach, and equity checks.

## Operationalization

- Define how OA publications count in hiring, promotion, tenure, or annual review.
- Measure baseline OA output before the assessment rule is implemented.
- Expand or document institutional fee support before treating OA publication as a rewarded behavior.
- Compare OA reach after implementation using local-community reach, citations, altmetrics, or other documented outcomes.
- Stratify by career stage, discipline, grant access, and fee support to detect resource-driven inequality.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Moher et al. (2018) states that if OA publishing becomes a responsible indicator for assessing scientists, a university could measure OA fractions before and after implementation and evaluate broader reach.
- The paper adds that before such implementation the university would need to expand its OA fund for faculty use.
- Moher et al. later state that if open access is included in faculty assessments, the institution would need to support open-access fees.

## Caveats

- OA rewards without fee support can favor well-funded researchers and institutions.
- OA status alone does not prove quality, reuse, reproducibility, or societal value.
- Green OA, Gold OA, and Bronze OA routes may imply different costs and incentives.
