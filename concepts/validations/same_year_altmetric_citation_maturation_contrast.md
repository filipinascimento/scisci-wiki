# Same-year altmetric-citation maturation contrast

## Summary

Same-year altmetric-citation maturation contrast is the validation check that immediate altmetric coverage can be compared with same-year citation coverage, while recognizing that citations continue to mature afterward.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: publication-year cohort, DOI-bearing paper, altmetric event, citation event, or impact indicator.
- Typical representation: same-year coverage comparison, maturation caveat, cohort-age contrast, or time-window validation table.
- Validation target: determine whether fast altmetric traces provide more coverage than early citation data for recent papers.
- Empirical signature: same-year citation presence can already match or exceed altmetric presence, and citation coverage will continue growing while immediate altmetric events may not.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines [altmetric-citation maturation asymmetry](altmetric_citation_maturation_asymmetry.md) with a concrete same-year coverage comparison.

- Provides a time-window guardrail for [altmetric-citation association](altmetric_citation_association.md) and [altmetric highly cited filtering](altmetric_highly_cited_filtering.md).

## Operationalization

- Select a fixed publication-year cohort, preferably DOI-bearing records when provider matching depends on DOI.
- Measure the share with at least one altmetric event in the same year.
- Measure the share with at least one citation in the same year and again after later citation windows mature.
- Report whether altmetric presence provides unique coverage, earlier coverage, or lower coverage than citation data for the intended use.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Costas et al. (2015) reports that in 2012 about 24% of DOI-bearing publications had altmetric scores, while 26% had already received at least one citation in the same year.
- The paper notes that citation counts for that cohort would naturally increase over subsequent months and years.
- It contrasts this with altmetric scores, which are immediate and not expected to increase substantially over time for the same publications.
- The comparison limits claims that altmetrics can replace citations for recent-paper evaluation on coverage grounds alone.

## Caveats

- Same-year citation coverage differs by field, publication month, database, and document type.
- Some altmetric sources may accumulate later than others.
- Coverage comparisons do not establish meaning or validity; they only address timing and denominator usefulness.
