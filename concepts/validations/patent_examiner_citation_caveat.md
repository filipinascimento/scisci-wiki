# Patent examiner citation caveat

## Summary

Patent examiner citation caveat marks the fact that patent citations are partly generated by examiners and legal strategy, so publication-style citation interpretations and self-citation adjustments do not transfer cleanly to patents.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: patent citation, patent-paper comparison, or paper/patent impact metric.
- Typical representation: data-quality caveat, separate patent-citation mechanism, or exclusion from publication-style self-citation removal.
- Mechanism or measurement target: examiner-added citations and applicant incentives change the meaning of patent citation links.
- Empirical signature: paper and patent citation analyses use different robustness rules or interpretive labels.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Qualifies [relative team-impact ratio](../measures/relative_team_impact_ratio.md) and [self-citation-adjusted team impact](self_citation_adjusted_team_impact.md) when patents are analyzed alongside papers.
- Links team-science measures to broader [patent citation source roles](patent_citation_source_roles.md).
- Connects to the broader [patent-to-patent citation noise](patent_to_patent_citation_noise.md) validation when patent-to-patent edges are interpreted as knowledge-flow evidence.
- Helps keep [WOS-USPTO team-production panel](../datasets/wos_uspto_team_production_panel.md) interpretations from treating publication and patent citations as identical evidence.
- Applies to science-technology linkage work using [integrated patent-paper citation networks](../representations/integrated_patent_paper_citation_networks.md).

## Operationalization

- Document whether patent citations were supplied by applicants, examiners, or both when the data source allows it.
- Avoid applying author-overlap self-citation removal rules to patent citations without a patent-specific rationale.
- Separate paper citation impact from patent citation impact in tables and robustness checks.
- State whether patent citations are used as knowledge-flow evidence, legal-document evidence, impact evidence, or a hybrid proxy.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Wuchty, Jones, and Uzzi (2007) warns that patent citation trends may be weaker because applicants may avoid citations and patent examiners add the majority of citations.
- In their self-citation note, Wuchty et al. do not remove patent self-citations because patent citations to prior work are primarily assigned by examiners.
- The paper uses this caveat to distinguish publication citation self-citation checks from patent citation analysis.

## Caveats

- Examiner assignment does not make patent citations uninformative, but it changes the causal interpretation of a citation edge.
- Applicant-supplied and examiner-supplied citation fields may not be available in older or harmonized patent datasets.
- Patent citation rules, disclosure duties, and examiner practices differ across patent offices and time periods.
