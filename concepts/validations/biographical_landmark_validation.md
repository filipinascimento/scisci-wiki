# Biographical landmark validation

## Summary

Metric outliers can be checked against disciplinary biography, prizes, eponyms, textbooks, and landmark narratives.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: metric outlier, landmark paper, prize citation, eponym, textbook construct, disciplinary history, or expert narrative.
- Typical representation: qualitative validation table for high-rank metric outliers.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: plausibility validation of recursive citation-ranking outliers.
- Empirical signature: high-PageRank outliers are more credible when external disciplinary memory recognizes them as landmarks..

## Operationalization

- Annotate PageRank outliers with external recognition anchors such as Nobel citations, named concepts, textbook constructs, or standard histories.
- Use qualitative validation to distinguish hidden gems from metric artifacts.
- Keep the validation separate from causal claims about metric correctness.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Chen et al. (2007) justifies high-PageRank outliers using recognizable cases including Onsager's Ising solution, Anderson localization, Wigner-Seitz cells, Glauber's Nobel-recognized work, and Dyson's QED synthesis.
- The motif records a qualitative validation layer for metric outliers.

## Caveats

- This validation is subjective and field-specific.
- It validates plausibility rather than causal metric correctness.
