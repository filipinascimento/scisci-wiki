# Discovery objective-function plurality

## Summary

Discovery efficiency depends on the objective function; novel-link discovery is only one possible target.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: objective function, novel link, repeated experiment, robustness, translation, error minimization, or usefulness outcome.
- Typical representation: objective-function menu for evaluating research strategies.
- Mechanism, measurement, or validation target: validity boundary of discovery-efficiency simulations.
- Empirical signature: strategies that optimize novel links may not optimize robustness, translation, or error correction..

## Uses in Science of Science

- Refines discovery-simulation validation by linking it to [knowledge network search efficiency](../measures/knowledge_network_search_efficiency.md) and [replication reproducibility](replication_reproducibility.md).
- Useful as a reusable distinction when [study power false positive risk](../measures/study_power_false_positive_risk.md) is analyzed in science-of-science data.
- Adds cross-links to [scientific discovery prediction](../methods/scientific_discovery_prediction.md) so the motif is not read as an isolated result.

## Operationalization

- Re-estimate or compare strategies under alternative objectives such as robustness, error minimization, usefulness, or diminishing returns to repeated links.
- State whether the objective rewards first discovery, confirmation, translation, or balanced portfolios.
- Use objective sensitivity to avoid universal policy claims from one search-efficiency metric.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from Rzhetsky et al. (2015) limits its efficiency measure to novel chemical relationships and notes that repetition can support medical and industrial technologies.
- The motif marks the objective boundary of optimized discovery simulations.

## Caveats

- Alternative objectives are proposed but not implemented in the core results.
- Policy conclusions can reverse when reliability or translational value is weighted more than first-link novelty.
