# Self-citation mechanism portfolio boundary

## Summary

Self-citation mechanism portfolio boundary is the validation caution that a descriptive demographic self-citation gap can be compatible with several mechanisms, and the observed data may not adjudicate among them.

## Canonical Form

- Unit of analysis: author, authorship, self-citation event, demographic group, field, or mechanism claim.
- Typical representation: mechanism checklist, competing-explanation table, or limitation statement attached to self-citation gap estimates.
- Validation target: prevent a self-citation gap from being attributed to one mechanism when multiple channels remain plausible.
- Empirical signature: evidence supports a gap but cannot distinguish preferences, self-promotion norms, specialization, productivity, authorship mode, or sanctions.

## Uses in Science of Science

- Bounds interpretation of [gendered self-citation gap](../mechanisms/gendered_self_citation_gap.md).
- Pairs with [self-citation opportunity-set confound](self_citation_opportunity_set_confound.md) when publication volume and career stage are not fully observed.
- Connects to [field gender-composition self-citation threshold check](field_gender_composition_self_citation_threshold_check.md) and [self-citation equity-intervention backlash risk](self_citation_equity_intervention_backlash_risk.md).
- Helps keep descriptive bibliometric evidence separate from causal claims about behavior.

## Operationalization

- List candidate mechanisms before interpreting group gaps.
- Identify which mechanisms are ruled out, partially tested, or not tested by the data.
- Report whether the design observes individual careers, paper types, authorship roles, field composition, and publication opportunities.
- Avoid policy prescriptions that assume one untested mechanism.

## Evidence and Validations

- Verified full-text evidence from King et al. (2017) discusses multiple possible explanations for the gendered self-citation gap.
- The paper frames its evidence as descriptive and explicitly discusses limits in adjudicating among self-assessment, sanction, specialization, productivity, and authorship-pattern channels.
- This motif is distinct from individual mechanism pages because it is the boundary condition around mechanism inference.
- It is especially relevant when the same aggregate gap can arise from different combinations of career, field, and behavior differences.

## Caveats

- A mechanism portfolio can become too broad to guide empirical design unless paired with testable contrasts.
- Some mechanisms can coexist and reinforce each other.
- Mechanism uncertainty does not negate the descriptive gap; it limits causal attribution.
